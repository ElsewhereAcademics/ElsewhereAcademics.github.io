Culinary Arts / Gastronomy

Culinary Arts / Gastronomy

Gastronomy and culinary arts programs combine advanced kitchen skills with knowledge of food safety, nutrition, costing and business management. The programs also cover topics such as product innovation, designing food and beverage experiences and leadership in the kitchen.

Sport and culinary arts

Example courses

Advanced culinary skillsFood safety and hygieneNutrition and healthCosting and financial managementInnovation and creativity in culinary products

What do graduates do?

  • Chef (head chef)
  • Sous chef
  • Restaurant owner or operator
  • Restaurant manager
  • Catering manager
  • Culinary arts instructor
  • Hotel food and beverage staff
IN HIGH SCHOOL

Which subjects matter?

Both schools expect a high school diploma and IELTS 5.5 or an equivalent English certificate.

Example: Munster Technological University, courses studied in the program

  1. Culinary Essentials
  2. Introduction to Management
  3. Introduction to Food Safety & Culinary Science
  4. Pastry & Bakery Skills Development
  5. Exploring Cuisines
  6. Menu Planning & Costings

Length by country

Ireland
MTU Culinary Leadership & Management: BA (Level 7, MT558) 3 years; BA (Hons) (Level 8, MT949) 4 years.
USA
A bachelor's degree is usually completed in 4 years of full-time study.
Netherlands
A bachelor's at a research university (WO) takes 3 years, 180 ECTS. At a university of applied sciences (HBO) it takes 4 years, 240 ECTS.
Germany
At a university, a bachelor's degree takes 3 years, 180 ECTS. At a Fachhochschule it usually takes 3 or 3.5 years, 180 or 210 ECTS.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a placement in gastronomy education?

In the two Swiss schools we looked at, a placement is part of the program. In BHMS's 3-year program, there is a 6-month placement in the second term of each year.

Which institution awards the diploma of a gastronomy bachelor's studied in Switzerland?

These two schools award a dual degree. At BHMS, the bachelor's is awarded together with Robert Gordon University in the United Kingdom.
