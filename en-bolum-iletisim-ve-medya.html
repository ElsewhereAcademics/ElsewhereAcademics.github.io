Communication and Media Studies

Communication and Media Studies

Communication and media studies brings together communication, culture and technology, and examines the role of media in society in both theory and practice. Digital culture, journalism, visual media, public relations and media ethics are among the main topics of the program.

IN HIGH SCHOOL

Which subjects matter?

The University of Leeds asks for AAB at A-level for Communication and Media (BA). One of these subjects must be from the arts, humanities or social sciences. General Studies and Critical Thinking are not counted.

Example: Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam, first-year courses

  1. Introduction to Communication Science
  2. Journalism: News and Information in a Changing World
  3. Interpersonal Communication: The Dynamics of Everyday Interactions
  4. Social Research Methodology
  5. Descriptive and Inferential Statistics
  6. Public Relations: Strategic Reputation Management

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can you do a placement year while studying media in the United Kingdom?

In many programs it is possible as an option. At Loughborough University, Media and Communication takes 3 years, or 4 years with a placement year, and the placement year gives an extra Diploma in Professional Studies. At the University of Leeds too, the optional placement year extends the degree by 12 months.

United Kingdom page Ask your question on WhatsApp
Is it possible to study in another country for a year during your bachelor's?

In these two programs, yes. At Loughborough, the optional year abroad gives an extra Diploma in International Studies; the placement and the year abroad can be split into six months each. At Leeds, the year abroad is also optional and is usually done as an extra academic year.
