ELSEWHERE?

Who are we?

We are Elsewhere Academics. We offer study abroad consulting. For high school, summer school, language school, bachelor's and master's, we guide the student to the most suitable school, and we walk through the process together, from application to departure.

PrepSummerCampusNext
FOUNDER

Berkay Yılmaz, the founder of Elsewhere Academics, has been doing study abroad consulting for more than 20 countries since 2022. He founded Elsewhere because he believes every student who goes abroad is a spark.
