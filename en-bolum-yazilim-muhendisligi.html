Software Engineering

Software Engineering

Software engineering focuses on the design, development, testing and maintenance of software. Programming languages, software architecture, web and mobile application development, and networks and operating systems are among the main topics.

Example courses

Programming languagesSoftware design and architectureWeb and mobile application developmentVirtualization and cloud computingNetworks and operating systems

What do graduates do?

  • Software engineer
  • Software architect
  • App developer
  • Mobile application developer
  • Software test specialist
  • IT project manager
  • Game developer
  • Systems analyst
IN HIGH SCHOOL

Which subjects matter?

Royal Holloway asks for one of computer science, mathematics or physics at A-level and a 6 (B) in GCSE mathematics for Computer Science (Software Engineering). The University of Kent's Software Engineering program does not require a specific A-level subject; it asks for at least a C/4 in GCSE mathematics.

Example: The University of Edinburgh, first-year courses

  1. Informatics 1: Introduction to Computation
  2. Informatics 1: Object Oriented Programming
  3. Linear Algebra 1
  4. Calculus and Algebra Fundamentals for Informatics (optional)

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is it possible to work in a company for a year while studying software engineering in the United Kingdom?

Many programs have this option. At Royal Holloway, the program takes 4 years when a Year in Industry is added. The University of Kent also offers a Year in Industry option on its Software Engineering program.

Which accreditation should you look at in a software engineering program?

In the United Kingdom, BCS accredits computing programs. The University of Kent shows its Software Engineering program as accredited by both BCS and the Engineering Council. We recommend checking this information on the program's own page before applying.

Is a software engineering degree earned abroad recognized in Türkiye?

To use it in Türkiye, you get recognition and equivalence (denklik) from YÖK (Türkiye's Council of Higher Education). Equivalence means your degree is found equal to its counterpart in Türkiye. The preliminary application is made through e-Devlet or the YÖK website.
