Computer Engineering / Computer Science

Computer engineering teaches algorithms, programming, data structures and computer systems. Students learn the basics of software development, databases, cybersecurity and artificial intelligence.

IN HIGH SCHOOL

Which subjects matter?

For computer science, Cambridge wants mathematics at A-level or at IB Higher Level. If your school offers Further Mathematics, it expects you to take that too. Physics is not required.

Example: Imperial College London, first-year courses

  1. Introduction to Computer Systems
  2. Introduction to Databases
  3. Introduction to Computer Architecture
  4. Discrete Mathematics, Logic and Reasoning
  5. Graphs and Algorithms
  6. Linear Algebra

Exams required for this field

TMUA United Kingdom

Cambridge Computer Science requires the TMUA. Applicants to Peterhouse or Trinity also sit the CSAT.

TMUA United Kingdom

Imperial requires the TMUA for its Computing department and for the joint Mathematics and Computing programs.

TOL, TOLC-I, CEnT-S or SAT Italy

Politecnico di Milano accepts one of these four exams for engineering bachelor's degrees for 2026/27. TOL and TOLC-I are taken in Italian, CEnT-S and SAT in English.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is a separate exam required when applying for computer science?

Some universities require one. Cambridge requires the TMUA (Test of Mathematics for University Admission) from all applicants to computer science. Imperial College London also sets a TMUA requirement for its Computing (Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning) program.

Can you study computer engineering in English in Europe?

In some countries, yes. The University of Twente in the Netherlands offers its Technical Computer Science bachelor's in English over 3 years. In Italy, Politecnico di Milano's Engineering of Computing Systems bachelor's is in Italian.

Ask your question on WhatsApp
Is a computer engineering degree earned abroad valid in Türkiye?

To use your degree in Türkiye, you need to get recognition and equivalence (denklik) from YÖK (Türkiye's Council of Higher Education). Recognition means that YÖK accepts that the school and the program are authorized to award degrees. The preliminary application is made through e-Devlet or the YÖK website.
