Ireland

The language of instruction in Ireland is English, and a bachelor's degree usually takes 3 to 4 years. Applicants from outside the EU ask each university separately whether to apply through the CAO or directly to the school. The Free Fees Initiative, under which the state pays bachelor's tuition, is for citizens of the EU, the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland.

IRELAND

Studying in Ireland

01

Applying for a bachelor's: mostly directly to the university

Students applying from Türkiye usually apply directly to the university for a bachelor's in Ireland, not through the CAO. Trinity College Dublin uses its own online application for non-EU applicants. At UCD, the student's fee status decides the route, and those with EU fee status go through the CAO. According to Trinity's 2027 guide, the High School Diploma is accepted for direct entry only from certain high schools.

source · source
02

School year, language and length

Classes are taught in English and a bachelor's usually takes 3 to 4 years. The university year runs from September to May, and exams are held in December and May. For a bachelor's, Trinity asks for an overall IELTS score of 6.5 and at least 6 in each section.

source · source
03

Tuition: Turkish students pay the non-EU fee

The Free Fees Initiative, under which the state pays bachelor's tuition, is for citizens of the EU, the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland. Turkish students pay the university's non-EU fee. For 2027/28 entry at Trinity, the annual fee for BESS (business, economic and social studies) and law is €23,250. At UCC, an Arts bachelor's costs €18,500 a year for 2026/27.

source · source
04

Working while studying and monthly costs in Dublin

A student with Stamp 2 permission can work up to 20 hours a week during term time. During the holiday periods, between June and September and between 15 December and 15 January, the limit is 40 hours a week. According to UCD's April 2026 estimate, a student's mid-range monthly cost in Dublin is €2,445, of which €1,200 is rent.

source · source
05

Staying on with Stamp 1G after graduation

After graduation you can get Stamp 1G permission under the Third Level Graduate Programme. Graduates with an honours bachelor's degree (level 8) get up to 12 months of extra permission. For master's and PhD graduates this goes up to 24 months and is given as 12 plus 12 months.

source · source

IRELAND

Key points for Ireland

01

At least 25 weeks and the ILEP list for language school

English programs that can be studied on a student permission must be on the ILEP list and last at least 25 weeks. The permission for these programs is given for 8 months. You can study a language for a total of two years with up to three permissions.

IN BRIEF

Ireland in brief

Language and summer schoolBachelor'sGeneral

Last checked: October 2026. Rules and amounts can change. We check the current situation together before you apply.

Language schools and summer schools

English programs that can be studied on a student permission must be on the ILEP list and last at least 25 weeks. The permission given for these programs is 8 months, and you can study a language for a total of two years with up to three permissions.

Bachelor's: length and application route

Bachelor's programs usually take 3 to 4 years. Applicants who live or have lived outside the EU should ask each higher education institution's admissions office whether to apply through the CAO or directly to the institution. The normal CAO application deadline is 1 February 2027.

Language of instruction

English is the main language of instruction in Ireland. Irish is taught in schools but is not required from international students.

Tuition fees

The Free Fees Initiative, which pays tuition, covers only citizens of the EU, the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland. Tuition fees vary by program and university.

Student visa

Full-time students stay in Ireland with a Stamp 2 student permission. Citizens of countries that need a visa must apply for a student visa before they arrive.

Proof of funds

Immediate access to at least 10,000 EUR must be shown for academic programs starting after 1 July 2023. For each following year, 10,000 EUR and the tuition fee are required. For stays shorter than 8 months, 833 EUR a month or 6,665 EUR in total is asked, whichever is lower.

Working while studying

Students with Stamp 2 permission can work up to 20 hours a week during term time and up to 40 hours a week during holidays (between June and September, and between 15 December and 15 January). Students with Stamp 2A permission cannot work.

Work permit after graduation

Under the Third Level Graduate Programme (Stamp 1G), graduates at level 8 (honours bachelor's) can get up to 12 months of extra permission, and graduates at level 9 and above (master's and PhD) up to 24 months.

Academic calendar

The university year usually runs from September to May, and exams are held in December and May.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do we need a visa for Ireland, and where do we apply?

Yes. Holders of Turkish passports, including green passports, need a visa to travel to Ireland. You first apply with the online AVATS form, and then hand in the documents to the VFS Global center in Türkiye.

source · source
Can I start a bachelor's directly with a high school diploma from Türkiye?

It depends on the school. According to Trinity College Dublin's 2027 guide, the High School Diploma is accepted for direct entry only from certain high schools, and the list is requested at study@tcd.ie. For other students, Trinity has an International Foundation Programme option, and the IELTS requirement for this program is at least 5.0.

source · source
Which visa do we need for a summer school?

For courses of 90 days or less, you get a C study visa. For a student under 18, a parent or legal guardian makes the application. This visa has no fixed minimum funds requirement, but you are asked to show money to cover costs with a bank statement.

source
Is it possible to study for a long time on a student permission at a language school?

Yes, but the program must be on the ILEP list and last at least 25 weeks. The permission for this course is given for 8 months, and you can study a language for a total of two years with up to three permissions. €6,665 is asked to be shown in the visa application.

source · source
Besides school fees and rent, what one-off costs are there?

€300 is paid for the first-year residence permit (IRP). Health insurance goes up to €690 according to UCD and up to about €550 according to Trinity. UCD residences also charge a €750 rent deposit.

source · source
