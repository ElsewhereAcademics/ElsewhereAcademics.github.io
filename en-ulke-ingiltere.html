UNITED KINGDOM

Studying in the United Kingdom

01

The route to a bachelor's: UCAS and five choices

A bachelor's application in the United Kingdom is made through UCAS, and you choose up to five programs in one application. A full-time bachelor's normally takes three years. In Scotland, an Honours degree takes four years. For 2027 entry, the deadline for Oxford, Cambridge and most medicine programs was 15 October 2026. For most other programs, the equal consideration deadline is 13 January 2027 at 18:00.

source · source
02

Child Student visa for high school

Students aged 4 to 17 apply with a Child Student visa to study at an independent (private) school. A CAS document from the school is needed before the visa application. You are asked to show money that covers the school and boarding fees for one academic year at a boarding school, meaning at most 9 months. Written consent from parents is required in every application.

source · source
03

A master's is often one year

Many master's programs in the United Kingdom are completed in one year. Postgraduate applications are not made through UCAS but through each university's own system, and there is no limit on the number of applications. Some programs have more than one start term a year.

source · source
04

Working while studying and money shown for the visa

A student at bachelor's level or above can work up to 20 hours a week during term, and full-time work is allowed during holidays. For programs below bachelor's level, the limit is 10 hours a week. For the Student visa, you show £1,529 a month in London and £1,171 a month outside London. This amount is calculated for up to 9 months.

source · source
05

Graduate visa after graduation

There is a Graduate visa for working after graduation. For applications made until 31 December 2026, the period is 2 years. From 1 January 2027, it is 18 months. Under today's rules, a student who starts a bachelor's in 2027 gets the 18-month period when they graduate. For PhD graduates, the period is 3 years.

UNITED KINGDOM

Important for the United Kingdom

01

Five choices and two separate deadlines

You choose up to five programs on the UCAS form. For 2027 entry, the deadline for Oxford, Cambridge and most medicine, dentistry and veterinary programs is 15 October 2026 at 18:00. For most other programs, the equal consideration deadline is 13 January 2027. Times are UK time.

02

The personal statement now answers three questions

From 2026 entry, the personal statement is no longer an open text. You answer three separate questions. The questions ask why you want the subject, how your courses have prepared you, and what you do outside school. The total limit is 4,000 characters including spaces, and each answer needs at least 350 characters. The same text goes to all your UCAS choices.

IN BRIEF

The United Kingdom in brief

High schoolLanguage and summer schoolBachelor'sMaster'sGeneral

Last checked: October 2026. Rules and amounts can change. We check the current situation together before you apply.

High school: international students

Students aged 4 to 17 can apply with a Child Student visa to study at an independent (private) school. For a boarding school, you need to show money that covers the school and boarding fees for one academic year (at most 9 months).

Language schools and summer schools

The United Kingdom has English language centers accredited by the British Council, and these centers have safeguarding procedures for students under 18. Holiday-period courses for teenagers and families are also offered. The Short-term study visa is used for English courses of six to eleven months.

Bachelor's: length and application route

A full-time bachelor's normally takes three years. In Scotland, an Honours degree takes four years. Applications are made through UCAS, and you can apply to up to five programs.

Master's: length and application route

Many master's programs can be completed in one year. Postgraduate programs have their own entry requirements and application processes. There is no limit on the number of applications, and there can be more than one start term a year.

Student visa

For higher education, the Student visa is used, and for independent school students aged 4 to 17, the Child Student visa. The visa application can be made at the earliest six months before the program starts.

Proof of funds

Funds of 1,529 GBP per month for programs in London and 1,171 GBP per month for programs outside London must be shown. The amount is calculated for up to 9 months. These are the current figures published on GOV.UK as of October 2026.

Working while studying

Students at bachelor's level and above can work up to 20 hours a week during term time. Students below bachelor's level can work up to 10 hours. Full-time work is allowed during holidays.

Work permit after graduation

The Graduate visa is valid for 2 years for applications made up to and including 31 December 2026, and for 18 months for applications made on or after 1 January 2027. For PhD graduates the period is 3 years.

Academic calendar

The academic year usually starts in September or October and runs until June or July. Postgraduate programs may have more than one start date a year.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do we need a visa for a summer school in the United Kingdom?

Yes. Türkiye is on the list of countries whose citizens need a visa in advance to travel to the United Kingdom for any purpose. Courses of up to 6 months, such as summer school, can be taken under the Standard Visitor route at an accredited institution.

source · source
When can we apply for a student visa, and how long does it take to get a result?

You first need the CAS document from the university to apply for the visa. You can apply 6 months before the course starts at the earliest. According to GOV.UK, applications made from outside the United Kingdom are usually decided within 3 weeks.

source · source
How much is the visa application fee?

According to the July and August 2026 copies of GOV.UK, the application fee from outside the United Kingdom is £558 for both the Student visa and the Child Student visa. On top of this fee, the immigration health surcharge (IHS) is paid at the time of application.

source · source
How long before the course starts can we travel to the United Kingdom?

For programs longer than 6 months, you can enter up to 1 month before the course starts with a Student visa. For programs of 6 months or less, this period is 1 week. We recommend that you do not set off before you have your visa in hand or before the start date.

source · source
Is VAT added to boarding school fees?

The three schools we checked publish their 2026/27 fees including VAT: Millfield, Whitgift and Rydal Penrhos. In Whitgift's 2025/26 table, the termly fee for full boarding is £16,475 excluding VAT and £19,770 including VAT. When you compare schools, we suggest checking whether the figure includes VAT.

source · source
