Sociology

Sociology

Sociology studies how societies work, human behavior and social change. Topics such as urban life, media, social movements and ethics are covered together with social research methods.

Example courses

The individual and societyUrban sociologySocial movements and protestMedia and crimeApplied ethics

What do graduates do?

  • Social researcher
  • Policy specialist
  • Community development specialist
  • Equality, diversity and inclusion specialist
  • Market researcher
  • NGO worker
  • Social work specialist
  • Teacher and academic
IN HIGH SCHOOL

Which subjects matter?

LSE does not require the subject sociology for sociology (BSc). It lists the typical choices as sociology, psychology, history, Government and Politics, religious studies and English, and it especially wants good grades in English language or literature.

Example: University College London, first-year courses

  1. Introduction to Sociology
  2. Historical Foundations of Sociological Thought
  3. Introduction to Social Science Research
  4. Social Change Within Contemporary Society
  5. Global Issues: Interconnections and Dislocations
  6. Discovering Qualitative and Quantitative Research Methods (DQQRM)

Length by country

United Kingdom
UCL Sociology BSc takes 3 years, or 4 years with a year abroad added.
USA
A bachelor's degree is usually completed in 4 years of full-time study.
Netherlands
A bachelor's at a research university (WO) takes 3 years, 180 ECTS. At a university of applied sciences (HBO) it takes 4 years, 240 ECTS.
Germany
At a university, a bachelor's degree takes 3 years, 180 ECTS. At a Fachhochschule it usually takes 3 or 3.5 years, 180 or 210 ECTS.

Exams required for this field

TARA United Kingdom

In 2026, UCL Sociology BSc asks all applicants for TARA (Test of Academic Reasoning for Admissions). The exam is run by University Admissions Tests UK.

College assessment United Kingdom

At Cambridge, sociology is studied within Human, Social, and Political Sciences; there is a college assessment.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is a sociology bachelor's in the Netherlands taught in English?

It depends on the university. The University of Amsterdam's sociology bachelor's is in English, takes 3 years and is 180 ECTS; there is also an option taught in a mix of Dutch and English. The University of Groningen's sociology bachelor's is taught in Dutch.

Netherlands page Ask your question on WhatsApp
Can you study sociology in English in Hungary?

Yes. The Sociology BA at ELTE (Eötvös Loránd University) in Budapest is taught in English, lasts 6 semesters and is 180 ECTS. The entrance exam is online: the applicant prepares a video of about 5 to 6 minutes on social issues.

Can you gain a year of work experience while studying sociology?

The University of Bath's BSc Sociology with Professional Placement takes 4 years, and the placement in the 3rd year is compulsory. Previous students did placements at organizations such as the Home Office, the Welsh Government and Government Social Research. There is no compulsory subject at A-level; the standard offer is AAB.
