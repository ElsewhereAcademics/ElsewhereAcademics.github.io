Architecture

Architecture

Architecture deals with the design of the spaces where people live and spend time. Programs cover architectural history and theory, building structure and construction, design and design communication, and digital technologies.

Example courses

Architectural history and theoryBuilding structure and constructionDesign and design communicationGraphics and modeling techniquesDigital built environment technologies

What do graduates do?

  • Architect
  • Architectural technologist
  • Urban designer
  • Landscape architect
  • Interior designer
  • Project manager
  • Sustainability consultant
IN HIGH SCHOOL

Which subjects matter?

Magdalene College at Cambridge does not require any specific A-level subject for architecture. Art (Fine Art) is preferred; maths, physics and a humanities subject are also seen as useful.

Exams required for this field

College assessment and portfolio United Kingdom

For Cambridge Architecture, all colleges run an assessment. An applicant invited to an interview brings a portfolio of recent work.

Portfolio United Kingdom

UCL Architecture BSc asks applicants invited at the application stage for a broad portfolio of creative work. It should not be sent unless asked for.

ARCHED Italy

The ARCHED test is required for the Politecnico di Milano Architectural Design bachelor's. For 2026/27, the test for English programs is on 23 July 2026 and for Italian programs on 24 July 2026.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can a student who studies architecture abroad work as an architect in Türkiye?

First, you get recognition and denklik from YÖK for the degree. In YÖK's procedures and principles, architecture is among the degree fields that allow practicing the profession. In these fields, completing courses, an internship or a project can be asked for during the denklik process.

source · source
Is an architecture bachelor's enough to become an architect in the United Kingdom?

Not on its own. ARB registration is needed for the title 'Architect'. This requires Part 1, Part 2, Part 3 and at least 2 years of professional experience. For those who start after September 2025, an ARB approved, master's level qualification is required.

source · source
How do the portfolio and interview work for Cambridge architecture applications?

Magdalene College asks for a PDF of up to 6 A4 pages, under 15 MB. Drawings based on observation are especially recommended; professional architectural drawings such as plans and sections are not wanted. The interview lasts 40 to 45 minutes, and the applicant brings sketchbooks and takes a written assessment.
