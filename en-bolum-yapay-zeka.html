Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence

Artificial intelligence bachelor's programs add topics such as machine learning, natural language processing and computer vision on top of a computer science and mathematics base. The ethical side of artificial intelligence is also covered in the programs.

  • Artificial intelligence solutions architect

  • Data analyst
IN HIGH SCHOOL

Which subjects matter?

Imperial College London asks for an A* in mathematics at A-level for Computing (Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning). Computer science and physics are not compulsory; they are among the recommended subjects.

Example: The University of Edinburgh, first-year courses

  1. Informatics 1: Introduction to Computation
  2. Informatics 1: Object Oriented Programming
  3. Linear Algebra 1
  4. Calculus and Algebra Fundamentals for Informatics (optional)

Length by country

United Kingdom
Edinburgh Artificial Intelligence BSc takes 4 years. There is no entry into the second year.
USA
A bachelor's degree is usually completed in 4 years of full-time study.
Netherlands
A bachelor's at a research university takes 3 years (180 ECTS). At an HBO (university of applied sciences) it takes 4 years (240 ECTS).

Exams required for this field

TMUA United Kingdom

At Imperial, the Computing department asks for the TMUA. At Cambridge, artificial intelligence is studied within the Computer Science program, and the TMUA is compulsory.

TMUA and CSAT United Kingdom

Cambridge Computer Science has a machine learning topic in the 1st year. The TMUA is compulsory when applying; Peterhouse and Trinity also ask for the CSAT.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can you study an artificial intelligence bachelor's in English in Europe?

There are examples in the Netherlands. The University of Groningen's BSc Artificial Intelligence takes 3 years and is in English. Maastricht University's Data Science and Artificial Intelligence bachelor's is also in English.

Is an extra exam required when applying for artificial intelligence?

At some schools, yes. Imperial College London asks for the TMUA exam for Computing (Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning). Cambridge also asks all applicants to computer science for the TMUA.

Is an artificial intelligence degree earned abroad recognized in Türkiye?

For bachelor's degrees from abroad, you get recognition and denklik (degree recognition) from YÖK (Türkiye's Council of Higher Education). Denklik means the diploma is found equivalent to its counterpart in Türkiye. The preliminary application is made through e-Devlet or YÖK's website.
