Aerospace Engineering

Aerospace Engineering

Aerospace engineering teaches the design, planning and testing of aircraft and spacecraft. Aerodynamics, flight mechanics, aircraft design and space systems are the main subjects.

Example courses

AerodynamicsFlight mechanicsAircraft designHeat transferSpace systems and mission analysis

What do graduates do?

  • Aerospace engineer
  • Mechanical engineer
  • Design engineer
  • Maintenance engineer
  • Materials engineer
  • Production manager
  • Academic researcher
IN HIGH SCHOOL

Which subjects matter?

For aeronautical engineering, Imperial College London asks for a high level of mathematics and physics in high school and only recommends Further Mathematics. TU Delft also looks for mathematics and physics in the diploma for its Aerospace Engineering bachelor's.

source · source

Example: TU Delft, first-year courses

  1. Introduction to Aerospace Engineering AB
  2. Aerospace Solid Mechanics
  3. Thermodynamics, Aircraft Propulsion and Performance
  4. Classical Physics
  5. Calculus I
  6. Programming and Digital Skills

Exams required for this field

Numerus fixus (limited places and selection) Netherlands

TU Delft's Aerospace Engineering BSc has a limited number of places. For 2026/27, it takes up to 440 new students. The application deadline is 15 January, followed by a selection process.

ESAT United Kingdom

Imperial's Aeronautics department requires the ESAT.

TOL, TOLC-I, CEnT-S or SAT Italy

For Politecnico di Milano engineering bachelor's degrees, one of these exams is required for 2026/27.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is an engineering degree from abroad recognized in Türkiye?

YÖK (Türkiye's Council of Higher Education) handles the recognition and denklik (degree recognition) procedures for bachelor's degrees from abroad. Denklik means the degree is treated as equal to Turkish degrees in terms of learning outcomes. The preliminary application is made through e-Devlet or the YÖK website.

source · source
Can you study aerospace engineering in English in the Netherlands?

Yes, TU Delft's Aerospace Engineering bachelor's is taught entirely in English. There is no Dutch requirement for this program. You need an IELTS Academic, TOEFL iBT, or Cambridge CPE or CAE result. Language certificates older than two years are not accepted.

source · source
Is it possible to work in industry for a year during your studies?

At Imperial College London, Aeronautical Engineering students can move to the Year in Industry program (H420) after the third year. To do this, the student must be on track for a 2:1 and must have found a suitable industry placement.
