Psychology

Psychology studies the human mind, behavior, thinking processes and emotions using scientific methods. Research methods, data analysis and scientific reasoning take an important place in the program.

IN HIGH SCHOOL

Which subjects matter?

UCL asks for A*AA at A-level for the psychology bachelor's (BSc). The A* and A grades must be in two of biology, chemistry, maths, physics and psychology.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Why do people ask about BPS accreditation when studying psychology in the United Kingdom?

To enter accredited master's and doctoral programs in the United Kingdom, you need the Graduate Basis for Chartered Membership (GBC). The GBC is gained with a bachelor's accredited by the British Psychological Society (BPS); UCL's psychology bachelor's has this accreditation. After a non-accredited bachelor's, the GBC can be gained through a BPS approved conversion program.

source · source
Do you need a master's to work as a psychologist?

In the United Kingdom, yes. According to the BPS, postgraduate training is required to become a Chartered Psychologist; the training needed depends on the chosen area of psychology. These programs usually ask for at least a 2:1 from a BPS accredited bachelor's.

source
Is there a psychology bachelor's in English in Europe?

Yes. In the Netherlands, the University of Groningen's psychology bachelor's in English takes 3 years (180 EC). The program takes 225 students and places are given by selection. Leiden University's psychology bachelor's is also in English and takes 3 years.

source · source
