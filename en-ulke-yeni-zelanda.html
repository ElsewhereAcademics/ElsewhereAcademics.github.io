New Zealand

New Zealand has eight universities: Auckland, Otago, Canterbury, Victoria University of Wellington, Waikato, Massey, Lincoln and Auckland University of Technology. A master's can take 1 year, 18 months or 2 years depending on the program. After graduation, the work visa can be for up to 3 years.

NEW ZEALAND

Studying in New Zealand

01

Which doors does a Turkish high school diploma open?

The University of Otago treats many foreign high school diplomas as equal to New Zealand's university entrance qualification. You check your country's status on the university's list. Auckland's foundation program, the Certificate in Foundation Studies, accepts the Devlet Lise Diploması (state high school diploma) in its 2025 entry table. For the 12-month option of this program, 70/100 is required in the 3rd year of high school.

02

The academic year starts at the end of February

The academic year in New Zealand starts at the end of February or in early March and ends in November. At Otago, the first semester of 2027 starts on 1 March and the second on 12 July. So you can also start university in July. At Canterbury, commerce and science bachelor's take 3 years, and engineering and law take 4 years.

03

Working 25 hours a week while studying

New student visas granted from 3 November 2025 allow up to 25 hours of work a week during term. The previous limit was 20 hours. Most student visas also allow full-time work in the summer holiday and planned breaks. There is no hour limit for research master's and doctoral students.

04

Money to show in the bank

For a student visa, you show living money of 20,000 NZD a year for an academic year or longer of study. If the study is shorter than a year, 1,667 NZD is required for each month. The University of Auckland says applying for the visa three months before travel gives the best chance.

05

After graduation

The Post Study Work Visa can give the right to work in New Zealand for up to 3 years after graduation. The length depends on the qualification you earn and your field.

NEW ZEALAND

Important for New Zealand

01

Living money: two separate amounts for university and school

For a student visa, you show at least 20,000 NZD for the first year's living costs. For students in years 1 to 13 of school, this amount is 17,000 NZD. For study shorter than a year, a lower amount is required.

02

Master's length depends on your previous degree

A master's in New Zealand can take 1 year, 18 months or 2 years. The length is decided by the program and the student's earlier qualifications. Each institution states its entry requirements on its own website.

03

Part-time work while studying, no limit for research students

Most student visa holders can work part-time while studying. Some can work full-time during planned holidays. There is no hour limit for research master's and doctoral students.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you need a student visa for a short English course in the summer holiday?

Courses of up to 3 months in total within 12 months can be taken on a visitor visa. For a longer course, a student visa is required. The summer holiday in Türkiye falls in the winter term in New Zealand. Courses are open all year.

Where do New Zealand universities rank internationally?

In the QS World University Rankings 2026, the University of Auckland is 65th, the University of Otago is 197th (equal) and Massey University is 230th (equal). All eight universities in the country are in the top 500 of this list.

How long does a master's take?

Depending on the program, it can take 1 year, 18 months or 2 years. For example, the Master of Commerce is 120 points at Otago and 180 points at Canterbury. The number of points directly affects the length and the fee.

Are we too late to start in February 2027?

Applications are still open at several universities: the last day from abroad is 15 November 2026 at Otago, 1 December 2026 at Canterbury, and 8 December 2026 at Auckland for most bachelor's programs. At Auckland, programs such as medicine, optometry and pharmacy closed on 1 July 2026. Massey's 31 October 2026 date is not a final deadline. Later applications are also considered if there is space.

How much does a year of living costs come to?

The University of Otago calculates the 40-week year at 24,439 NZD for a student living in a campus flat in Dunedin, based on 2025 costs. This amount includes rent, food, personal expenses and insurance. According to Study with New Zealand, the yearly estimate for Victoria and Auckland is between 20,000 and 27,000 NZD.
