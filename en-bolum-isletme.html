Business Management / Business Administration

Business Management / Business Administration

The business field looks broadly at how organizations are managed and how the business world works. It gives you basic knowledge in areas such as strategy, leadership, finance, marketing and human resources, along with planning and problem-solving skills.

Business and economics

Example courses

Management theoriesStrategyMarketingFinanceEntrepreneurship

What do graduates do?

  • Business analyst
  • Management consultant
  • Business development manager
  • Project manager
  • Risk manager
  • Supply chain manager
  • Marketing specialist
  • Human resources specialist
IN HIGH SCHOOL

Which subjects matter?

The University of Bath asks for at least a 6 (B) in GCSE maths and English for BSc Business, 2026 entry. Rotterdam School of Management treats strong maths as a requirement for the IBA program; depending on your diploma, a maths proficiency test may be required.

Example: University College London, first-year courses

  1. The Art and Science of Management
  2. Data Analytics I (Statistics and Data Mining)
  3. Mathematics I (Calculus and Modelling)
  4. Business Economics I
  5. Critical Analytical Thinking

Length by country

United Kingdom
UCL Management Science BSc, 3 years.
USA
A bachelor's degree is usually completed in 4 years of full-time study.
Netherlands
A bachelor's at a research university (WO) takes 3 years, 180 ECTS. At a university of applied sciences (HBO) it takes 4 years, 240 ECTS.
Germany
At a university, a bachelor's degree takes 3 years, 180 ECTS. At a Fachhochschule it usually takes 3 or 3.5 years, 180 or 210 ECTS.

Exams required for this field

TARA United Kingdom

UCL Management Science BSc asks all applicants for the TARA in the 2026 cycle. The test is run by University Admissions Tests UK.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is it possible to gain work experience while studying business in the United Kingdom?

In some programs, a placement is part of the degree. In the University of Bath's BSc Business, students do two six-month placements at two different companies. The first is in year 2 and the second in year 3, so the program takes 4 years.

United Kingdom page Ask your question on WhatsApp
Can you study business in English in the Netherlands?

Yes. Rotterdam School of Management's International Business Administration (IBA) bachelor's is entirely in English. The program has limited places (numerus fixus) and takes 650 students for September entry. For 2027/28, the last day for Studielink registration is 15 January 2027.
