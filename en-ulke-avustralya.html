Australia

To study in Australia you need a Student visa (subclass 500). Since 10 May 2024, this visa requires savings of at least 29,710 AUD for one student. At universities the year is split into two semesters. The first starts in February or March, and the second in July or August.

AUSTRALIA

Studying in Australia

01

Is there direct entry with a Turkish high school diploma?

The University of Melbourne and the University of Sydney do not list the Turkish high school diploma among the diplomas they accept for direct entry. For diplomas not on the list, both universities point to a foundation program as the way in. Melbourne accepts the US high school diploma together with GPA and an SAT or ACT result. The IB Diploma and A Level are also on the list.

02

The year starts in February, with a second intake in July

At universities the year is split into two semesters. At the University of Queensland, the first semester of 2027 starts on 22 February and the second on 26 July. A bachelor's degree usually takes three years, or four with an Honours degree. A master's degree takes one to two years.

03

Public schools for high school and the under-18 rule

New South Wales public schools ask for 21,000 AUD a year for Years 11 and 12 in 2027, and 535 AUD a week for homestay accommodation. In Queensland the 2027 fee for the same years is 19,416 AUD, and homestay is 395 AUD a week. A student under 18 must have an approved welfare arrangement for the visa. The school can provide this with a CAAW document.

04

Working while studying and budget

On a student visa you can work up to 48 hours per fortnight during study periods. There is no hour limit during scheduled breaks. Since 10 May 2024, the visa requires savings of at least 29,710 AUD a year for one student. ANU calculates weekly spending in Canberra in a shared house at about 503 AUD for 2026.

05

The 485 visa after graduation

Graduates can stay in Australia with a Temporary Graduate visa (subclass 485), usually for 2 to 3 years depending on the qualification level. Since 1 July 2024, you must be 35 or under when applying for this visa. There are some exceptions.

AUSTRALIA

Key points for Australia

01

Two intakes in 2027

At Australian Catholic University, the first semester of 2027 starts on 1 March and the second on 2 August. The University of Melbourne shows its first semester as February to July and its second as July to December. The exact start of classes varies by university.

IN BRIEF

Australia in brief

High schoolLanguage and summer schoolBachelor'sMaster'sGeneral

Last checked: October 2026. Rules and amounts can change. We check the current situation together before you apply.

High school: international students

Public and private schools accept international students aged five and over. Students under 18 must have an approved welfare arrangement: a parent or legal guardian, a relative over 21, or a CAAW document arranged by the school.

Language schools and summer schools

ELICOS (English Language Intensive Courses for Overseas Students) courses are subject to national standards and an industry-based quality assurance framework. These courses start throughout the year and can be taken on a Student visa.

Bachelor's: length and application route

A bachelor's degree usually takes three years, or four with an Honours degree. You apply directly through the institution's website or through an education consultant that the institution supports.

Master's: length and application route

Postgraduate programs usually take one to two years. As with bachelor's degrees, you apply directly to the institution.

Student visa

A Student visa (subclass 500) is needed to study in Australia, and the application documents are uploaded through ImmiAccount. Applicants under 18 must have an approved welfare arrangement.

Proof of funds

For student visa applications made on or after 10 May 2024, you must show 29,710 Australian dollars a year for one student.

Working while studying

Student visa holders can work up to 48 hours per fortnight during study periods. There is no hour limit during scheduled breaks.

Work permit after graduation

The Temporary Graduate visa (subclass 485) usually gives the right to stay for 2 to 3 years, depending on the qualification level. You must be 35 or under at the time of application. There are some exceptions.

Academic calendar

University programs usually start in March. At schools the teaching year opens in late January or early February. ELICOS courses start throughout the year. The second semester starts in July or August.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much is the Australian student visa fee?

For the Student visa (subclass 500), the main applicant's fee has been 2,500 AUD since 1 July 2026. Before that date it was 2,000 AUD. The application is made online through ImmiAccount.

What documents are asked for in the visa application?

Study Australia explains the process step by step: offer letter, CoE, proof of English proficiency, the Genuine Student requirement and financial capacity. OSHC health insurance and health and character requirements are added to these. You show savings of at least 29,710 AUD a year for one student.

When can a high school student travel to Australia?

If the school issues a CAAW, the student cannot enter the country before the start date written on the document. The school reports this date to Home Affairs. If the family will go as guardians, Form 157N is added to the application.

What else is paid for accommodation and insurance at a public high school?

For 2027, New South Wales public schools ask for 535 AUD a week for homestay accommodation and 480 AUD for placement. The same program lists 12 months of OSHC health insurance at 821 AUD for 2027. For the school uniform, it gives an amount between 350 and 450 AUD.

How long is a language exam result considered valid?

UNSW College asks for IELTS, TOEFL and PTE results to be taken within 24 months before the enrollment date. The proof of English proficiency also appears as a separate step in Study Australia's visa process.
