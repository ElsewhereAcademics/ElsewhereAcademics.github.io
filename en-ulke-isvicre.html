Switzerland

There is no central application system in Switzerland, and each university sets its own admission requirements. At ETH Zurich, a student who earned the right to enter abroad has paid CHF 2,190 per semester in tuition since autumn 2025. A non-EU student can work while studying only after six months and for up to 15 hours a week.

SWITZERLAND

Studying in Switzerland

01

What opens the door: a high school diploma and, if needed, an exam

There is no central application system in Switzerland, and each university sets its own admission requirements. ETH Zurich decides whether an applicant with a foreign high school diploma needs an exam after seeing the full file. The University of St. Gallen (HSG) selects international bachelor's applicants with an online aptitude test and a video interview.

source · source
02

The language of study

At ETH Zurich, the main language of bachelor's programs is German, and some courses are taught in English or French. Most master's programs are in English. ETH asks that TOEFL, IELTS and C1 Advanced certificates are not older than two years.

source · source
03

A school year and program lengths

A full-time bachelor's takes three years. A master's takes one and a half to two years and requires 90 to 120 ECTS. The autumn semester runs from September to December, and the spring semester from February to May.

source · source
04

What a year in Zurich costs

ETH Zurich has charged foreign students CHF 2,190 per semester since autumn 2025. ETH estimates that rent, food, transport and insurance in Zurich come to about CHF 22,100 a year. The yearly rent average in this table is CHF 11,300 and is 2024 data.

source · source
05

Working while studying and after graduation

A student from outside the EU can work six months after starting studies and with cantonal approval. During term time the limit is 15 hours a week, and during holidays full-time work is allowed. A graduate can look for a job in Switzerland for six months after the end of studies.

source · source

SWITZERLAND

Key points for Switzerland

01

Foreign student tuition tripled at ETH

At ETH Zurich, tuition is CHF 730 per semester for Swiss citizens and for those who earned the right to enter while living in Switzerland. Other students pay CHF 2,190 per semester from the autumn 2025 semester. The ETH Board announced the increase, and parliament changed the ETH Act accordingly.

02

Apply directly to the university

In Switzerland, you apply for a bachelor's directly to the university, and a high school diploma recognized as equivalent to the Swiss Matura is required. A full-time bachelor's takes three years and a master's one and a half to two years.

03

Six months' wait before working

Students from outside the EU and EFTA can work with approval from the cantonal authorities and six months after starting studies. They can work up to 15 hours a week during term time and full-time during holidays. The employer must first request a work permit.

IN BRIEF

Switzerland in brief

Bachelor'sMaster'sGeneral

Last checked: October 2026. Rules and amounts can change. We check the current situation together before you apply.

Bachelor's: length and application route

A full-time bachelor's takes three years. There is no central application system. You apply directly to the university, and a high school diploma accepted as equivalent to the Swiss Matura is required.

Master's: length and application route

A full-time master's takes one and a half to two years and requires 90 to 120 ECTS. You apply directly to the university, and a bachelor's degree from an accredited university in a related field is required.

Tuition fees

Tuition fees vary by institution and are between CHF 500 and 4,000 per semester. Some institutions charge foreign students more: at ETH Zurich, foreign students have paid three times the fee of domestic students since autumn 2025.

Student visa

Students from outside the EU and EFTA apply for the visa at the Swiss diplomatic or consular representation in their country of residence.

Working while studying

Students from outside the EU and EFTA can start working with approval from the cantonal authorities and six months after studies begin. They can work up to 15 hours a week during term time and full-time between terms.

Work permit after graduation

Non-EU and non-EFTA students who graduate from a higher education institution in Switzerland can look for a job for six months after the end of studies.

Academic calendar

The autumn semester runs from September to December, and the spring semester from February to May.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I apply to ETH Zurich with a high school diploma from Türkiye?

Yes. ETH tells you whether an exam is needed after reviewing the full file from eApply. The shortened exam has 4 subjects and the comprehensive exam has 8. For autumn 2027, registration for the January 2027 exam closed on 15 October 2026.

source · source
How much money do we need to show for a student visa?

In the Canton of Zurich, a student shows CHF 21,000 for twelve months. This money is documented with a bank confirmation or a guarantor's declaration. According to ZHAW, the bank giving the confirmation must be recognized by FINMA.

source · source
Where and when do we apply for the long-stay student visa?

A student who will stay longer than 90 days applies for the national D visa, and you contact the Consulate General in Istanbul for an appointment. The permit depends on the approval of the migration office of the canton you will go to. Schools recommend applying at least three months before entry.

source · source
Which visa is needed for a summer camp?

For stays of up to 90 days, you get a Schengen visa. Those living in Türkiye apply at the VFS Global office in Istanbul or Ankara. Because of heavy demand, the Consulate General recommends booking the appointment up to 6 months before departure. Processing can take up to 15 days.

source · source
For how many years is the residence permit given?

For studies lasting several years, the permit is given for one year and renewed each year. To renew, the student must still be in education. In Geneva, this program must include at least 20 hours of classes a week.

source
