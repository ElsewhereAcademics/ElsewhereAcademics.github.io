Urban and Regional Planning

Urban and Regional Planning

Urban and regional planning deals with planning the development and renewal of cities and local communities. The program gives knowledge in areas such as urban design, planning processes, planning law and housing policy.

Example courses

Urban design and place makingPlanning processes and planning lawHousing policyUrban and regional planningUrban regeneration

What do graduates do?

  • Town planner
  • Transport planner
  • Urban designer
  • Local government specialist
  • Housing manager
  • Historic building conservation specialist
  • Sustainability consultant

Example: University College London (UCL), first-year courses

  1. Introducing Planning Systems
  2. Planning History and Thought
  3. Contemporary Cities
  4. Urban Lab I: Graphic Communication
  5. Introducing Urban Design: Design Skills
  6. Making Cities: The Production of the Built Environment

Length by country

United Kingdom
UCL Urban Planning and Real Estate BSc, 3 years. The program is accredited by RTPI and RICS.
USA
A bachelor's degree is usually completed in 4 years of full-time study.
Netherlands
A bachelor's at a research university takes 3 years (180 ECTS). At an HBO (university of applied sciences) it takes 4 years (240 ECTS).
Germany
At a university, a bachelor's degree takes 3 years, 180 ECTS. At a Fachhochschule it usually takes 3 or 3.5 years, 180 or 210 ECTS.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is a planning bachelor's enough to become a town planner in the United Kingdom?

Often it is not enough on its own. Bachelor's programs usually receive partial accreditation from the RTPI; after that, an RTPI approved master's or a complementary qualification is needed. Fully accredited graduates can move to Licentiate membership and reach full status with about 1 to 2 years of work experience.

source · source
Is there a placement year option in a planning bachelor's?

In some programs, yes. Cardiff University's Urban Planning and Development with a Professional Placement Year takes 4 years. The student spends one year working in a private or public organization; the program receives partial accreditation from the RTPI.
