Civil Engineering

Civil Engineering

Civil engineering deals with the design, construction and maintenance of the built environment. Fluid mechanics, soil mechanics, structural mechanics and engineering geology are core topics.

Example courses

Fluid mechanicsSoil mechanicsStructural mechanicsEngineering geologyGeomechanics

What do graduates do?

  • Civil engineer
  • Structural engineer
  • Site engineer
  • Water engineer
  • Environmental engineer
  • Construction supervision specialist
  • Quantity surveyor (quantity and cost specialist)
  • Construction manager
IN HIGH SCHOOL

Which subjects matter?

Imperial College London asks for A* in maths and A* or A in physics at A-level for Civil Engineering MEng. For the IB, it expects 7 in Higher Level maths and 6 in physics.

source

Exams required for this field

ESAT United Kingdom

Imperial's Civil and Environmental Engineering department requires the ESAT.

ESAT United Kingdom

At Cambridge, civil engineering topics are studied within the Engineering program; the ESAT is required to apply to this program.

TOL, TOLC-I, CEnT-S or SAT Italy

For Politecnico di Milano engineering bachelor's degrees, one of these exams is required for 2026/27.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can a civil engineer who studied abroad work in Türkiye?

First, you need to get recognition and denklik from YÖK for your degree. Recognition means accepting that the school and the program are authorized to award degrees. The preliminary application is made through e-Devlet or the YÖK website.

source · source
Which accreditation matters for civil engineering in the United Kingdom?

Civil engineering programs are accredited by the Joint Board of Moderators (JBM) on behalf of the Engineering Council. A JBM accredited MEng fully meets the education requirement for Chartered Engineer (CEng). A BEng meets it only in part, and further learning is needed. Imperial College London's Civil Engineering MEng is accredited in this way.

source · source
Can you study civil engineering in English in Europe?

At some schools, yes. The 3-year Civil Engineering bachelor's at Politecnico di Milano's Milano Leonardo campus can be studied in Italian or in English.

source
