Biology / Biological Sciences

Biology / Biological Sciences

Biology studies the structure, functioning and interactions of living things, from cells to ecosystems. Ecology, genetics, molecular biology, biochemistry and physiology are core subjects.

What do graduates do?

  • Research scientist (life sciences)
  • Microbiologist
  • Biotechnologist
  • Laboratory technician
  • Marine biologist
  • Conservation officer
  • Pharmacologist
  • Science teacher
IN HIGH SCHOOL

Which subjects matter?

For Biological Sciences, Imperial College London asks for an A in biology at A-level, plus an A in one of physics, chemistry or mathematics. In the IB, you need a 6 at Higher Level in biology and in one of chemistry, mathematics or physics (2027 entry).

Example: University College London (UCL), first-year courses

  1. Biochemistry and Molecular Biology
  2. Introduction to Genetics
  3. Cells and Development
  4. Life on Earth
  5. Methods in Ecology and Evolution
  6. Quantitative Biology

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is there a YKS requirement for the equivalence (denklik) of a biology degree earned abroad?

The YKS (Türkiye's university entrance exam) ranking requirement set by YÖK (Türkiye's Council of Higher Education) applies only to medicine, dentistry, pharmacy and law degrees. People who study other programs such as biology are exempt from this requirement. For your degree, you still need to get recognition and equivalence (denklik) from YÖK. The preliminary application is made through e-Devlet or the YÖK website.

Can you work in industry for a year while studying biology in the United Kingdom?

At some schools, yes. The University of Manchester's BSc Biology with Industrial/Professional Experience takes 4 years, and the 3rd year is spent in work experience. At the University of East Anglia, the 3rd year is also a placement in industry of 9 to 12 months, and students find the placement themselves. Both programs are accredited by the Royal Society of Biology.

Can you study biology in English in Europe?

Yes, in Hungary. The University of Pécs's Biology BSc is in English and takes 6 semesters, which is 3 years. The University of Debrecen also has a Biology BSc that is in English, with 6 semesters and 180 ECTS.
