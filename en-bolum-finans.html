Finance

The finance field studies financial markets, corporate finance, banking and investment. Financial reporting, budgeting and financial analysis skills form the core of the program.

  • Management consultant
IN HIGH SCHOOL

Which subjects matter?

For September 2027 entry to Accounting and Finance BSc, Warwick Business School asks for at least an A in mathematics at A-level (typical offer A*AA). The school states clearly that statistics, economics, accounting and finance courses do not count in place of the mathematics requirement.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does a finance bachelor's linked to the CFA exempt you from the CFA exams?

The CFA Institute's University Affiliation program recognizes bachelor's degrees whose curriculum is aligned with the CFA Candidate Body of Knowledge. According to the University of Technology Sydney, this alignment must be above 70%. The same school writes that its courses do not count as credit toward the CFA program, and all three levels must be passed by exam.

source · source
Are there programs where a year of work experience is compulsory while studying finance?

Yes. Warwick Business School's Accounting and Finance with Placement Year program takes 4 years. In this program, a year of placement in industry or a year abroad is compulsory.
