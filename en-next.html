EXAM CORNER

Which exams?

The exams most often asked for in applications. Each school decides which exam it asks for and with what score.

IELTS AcademicBritish Council, IDP IELTS and Cambridge University Press & Assessment

Asked for as proof of English proficiency in bachelor's and master's applications abroad.

Duration
2 hours 45 minutes (Listening 30, Reading 60, Writing 60, Speaking 11 to 14 minutes). Speaking can be on a different day.
Score
Each section and the overall score are bands from 1 to 9; 0 means the exam was not taken.
Validity
2 years from the exam date
Where
In Türkiye, at test centers on a computer in many cities including Istanbul, Ankara and İzmir. In some countries there is an option to take IELTS Online from home.
Official website
TOEFL iBTETS

Universities abroad ask for it as proof of English proficiency in bachelor's and master's applications.

Duration
About 2 hours. The time can vary slightly because the reading and listening sections are adaptive.
Score
Since 21 January 2026, each section and the total are scored from 1 to 6, in half-point steps. For two years after January 2026, an equivalent score from 0 to 120 is also given.
Validity
2 years from the exam date
Where
On a computer at a test center or at home (TOEFL iBT Home Edition, with a live proctor).
Official website
GRE General TestETS

Some master's, business and law programs ask for it in applications.

Duration
About 1 hour 58 minutes (Analytical Writing 30 minutes, two Verbal and two Quantitative sections)
Validity
5 years
Where
At a test center or at home; the home exam is open every day of the week, at any hour of the day.
Fee
$249 for Türkiye, excluding taxes (from 1 August 2026)
Official website
GMATGMAC

Some master's programs in business (MBA and similar) ask for it in applications.

Duration
2 hours 15 minutes and an optional 10-minute break. Three sections: Quantitative Reasoning, Verbal Reasoning and Data Insights, 45 minutes each.
Score
Total from 205 to 805; each section from 60 to 90
Validity
5 years from the exam date
Where
At a test center or online (from home)
Official website

ELSEWHERENEXT FAQ

Frequently asked questions

All questions

What should our grade average be for a master's?

UCL gives the Türkiye equivalent of a 2:1 degree in the United Kingdom as 3.0 out of 4 or 75%. For programs that ask for a 2:2, UCL's Türkiye equivalent is 2.7 out of 4 or 70%. The University of Edinburgh asks for 2.8 out of 4 or 75% for a 2:1; the threshold for the same degree can vary from school to school.

Ask your question on WhatsApp
Is work experience required for an MBA?

The Oxford Saïd MBA asks for at least two years of full-time work experience and expects it to be shown on a one-page résumé. For the Cambridge Judge MBA too, at least two years of full-time experience must be completed by the date the program starts. Both schools ask for a GMAT or GRE result; Oxford expects two references, and Cambridge expects a reference from a manager.

Ask your question on WhatsApp
Does a master's take one year or two?

In the United Kingdom, many master's programs are completed in one year. In the Netherlands, at research universities the length is at least one year; in engineering, mathematics and natural sciences it is two years. In Estonia, a master's is between 60 and 120 ECTS and as a rule takes two years.

Ask your question on WhatsApp
Can we apply for a master's before the bachelor's is finished?

A student who has completed at least two thirds of the bachelor's credits can apply to the physics master's at Freie Universität Berlin. The diploma document must be submitted before the end of the first semester of the master's. UCL gives a conditional offer based on the diploma; for the start on 28 September 2026, it asked for documents to be uploaded by 11 September 2026.

Ask your question on WhatsApp
How many universities can we apply to for a master's in the United Kingdom?

There is no limit on the number for postgraduate applications; applications are generally not made through UCAS but through each university's own system. Some programs have more than one start term per year.

JOURNEY

How do we work?

Eight steps, from the first meeting to the first week after the student arrives.

  1. 1

    First meeting

    We listen first. We do not talk about schools before we understand the student's interests, goals and the family's expectations.

  2. 2

    Roadmap

    After setting the goal, we draw the path to it. We share country, field of study and school options with you, together with why we suggest each one.

  3. 3

    Budget plan

    We put the paths that lead to the same goal with different budgets side by side. We make clear from the start which payment is due when.

  4. 4

    Exams and documents

    We build the calendar of exams the schools ask for and prepare the documents step by step.

  5. 5

    Applications

    We prepare the file each school asks for in full and complete the applications according to each school's own calendar. We regularly share with you which document was sent to which school and when.

  6. 6

    Decision

    When acceptances arrive, we put the options side by side and make the final decision together, keeping the student's goal in mind.

  7. 7

    Orientation

    Orientation starts with the acceptance. We prepare the student for the new city, the school culture and the first weeks. Depending on your package, visa, accommodation and travel planning are also part of this stage.

  8. 8

    After arrival

    The first mornings in a new city feel a little strange. At the end of the first week, we have a meeting with our student: classes, housing, new friends, suggestions.
