Elsewhere?

Who are we?

We are Elsewhere Academics. We offer study abroad consulting. For high school, summer school, language school, bachelor's and master's, we guide the student to the most suitable school, and we walk through the process together, from application to departure.

FOUNDER

Berkay Yılmaz

I have been doing study abroad consulting since 2022; I guide students and families toward schools in more than 20 countries.

In these years I often noticed one thing: studying abroad is often treated like a transaction that ends with an acceptance letter. But an acceptance letter is not an end, it is a beginning.

Every student who sets out is a spark. They grow with what they learn where they go, the people they meet and the life they build. One day that spark becomes a strong flame. I founded Elsewhere Academics to carry this spark to the right place.

Elsewhere means "another place". Every student has another place of their own, and our job is to find that place. We choose the right school, the right city and the right time together. We stay by the student's side after they arrive too. Before setting out, every student of ours receives a letter from their consultant; that day is the day the spark sets out.

How do we work?

Eight steps, from the first meeting to the first week after the student arrives.

1

First meeting

We listen first. We do not talk about schools before we understand the student's interests, goals and the family's expectations.

2

Roadmap

After setting the goal, we draw the path to it. We share country, field of study and school options with you, together with why we suggest each one.

3

Budget plan

We put the paths that lead to the same goal with different budgets side by side. We make clear from the start which payment is due when.

4

Exams and documents

We build the calendar of exams the schools ask for and prepare the documents step by step.

5

Application

We prepare the file each school asks for in full and complete the applications according to each school's own calendar. We regularly share with you which document was sent to which school and when.

6

Decision

When acceptances arrive, we put the options side by side and make the final decision together, keeping the student's goal in mind.

7

Orientation

Orientation starts with the acceptance. We prepare the student for the new city, the school culture and the first weeks. Depending on your package, visa, accommodation and travel planning are also part of this stage.

8

After arrival

The first mornings in a new city feel a little strange. At the end of the first week, we have a meeting with our student: classes, housing, new friends, suggestions.
