Chemical Engineering

Chemical Engineering

Chemical engineering designs processes that change the chemical, biochemical or physical state of materials, and deals with turning basic raw materials into everyday products. Thermodynamics, separation processes, fluid mechanics and process design are the core courses.

Example courses

ThermodynamicsSeparation processesHeat, mass and momentum transferReactor engineeringProcess design and analysis

What do graduates do?

  • Chemical engineer
  • Process development specialist
  • Energy engineer
  • Environmental engineer
  • Materials engineer
  • Biotechnologist
  • Food technologist
  • Sustainability consultant
IN HIGH SCHOOL

Which subjects matter?

Imperial College London asks for A* in chemistry and maths at A-level for Chemical Engineering MEng. The third subject can be physics, biology, economics, business or Further Mathematics. The University of Bath also asks for A-level chemistry and maths for chemical engineering.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is a placement year part of a chemical engineering program?

In the United Kingdom, some schools offer it as an option. The University of Bath's Chemical Engineering BEng with professional placement takes 4 years, and the placement is in year 3. According to Bath, the placement year also counts toward becoming a Chartered Engineer.

source · source
Why does accreditation of a chemical engineering program matter?

The Washington Accord is an agreement between bodies that accredit engineering bachelor's programs. ABET in the US, the Engineering Council in the United Kingdom, Engineers Canada in Canada and MÜDEK in Türkiye are part of this agreement. Member bodies treat programs accredited by each other as equal.

source · source
Is a chemical engineering degree earned abroad valid in Türkiye?

To use the degree in Türkiye, you need to get recognition and denklik from YÖK. Recognition means accepting that the school and the program are authorized to award degrees. The preliminary application is made through e-Devlet or the YÖK website.

source · source
