Latvia

LATVIA

Studying in Latvia

01

How does applying work?

Application, exam and interview steps run through the university you choose. At Riga Business School, every applicant takes the RBS Math Entrance Exercise, which tests high school algebra only. For English, at least B2 level is asked, for example IELTS 6.0. The University of Latvia also asks for proof of English proficiency. Applicants who studied in English in some countries are exempt.

02

Program length and calendar

An academic bachelor's degree is 180 to 240 ECTS credits and a master's degree is 120 ECTS credits. Study in Latvia gives the bachelor's as 3 to 4 years. At Rīga Stradiņš University, English-taught medicine takes 6 years and the International Business bachelor's takes 3 years. At RSU, classes for students entering in September 2027 start on 1 September 2027. Medicine and dentistry also have a February intake. The February 2027 term opens on 2 February.

03

Examples from 2026/27 tuition

At RSU, English-taught medicine for 2026/2027 is €13,500 a year and dentistry is €15,500. Students who start these two programs also pay a one-time registration fee of €1,800. In Riga Business School's business bachelor's, a non-EU student pays €7,550 a year for 2026/2027.

04

Working while studying

A full-time bachelor's student can work up to 20 hours a week during the term and up to 40 hours a week during holidays. There is no hour limit for full-time master's and doctoral students.

05

After graduation

The residence permit is given for the length of study plus 4 months after it ends. Master's and doctoral graduates can ask for a 9-month extension to look for a job. This request is made before the current permit ends, and approval is not certain.

What matters for Latvia

01

20 hours a week for a bachelor's, no limit for a master's

Bachelor's: length and application route

An academic bachelor's degree is 180 to 240 ECTS and a professional bachelor's is 240 ECTS. Study in Latvia states bachelor's programs as 3 to 4 years. Application, exam and interview steps run through the chosen university. The English-taught medicine program at Rīga Stradiņš University takes 6 years.

Master's: length and application route

Academic and professional master's programs are 120 ECTS. You apply to the chosen university.

Language of instruction

Latvia has programs taught in English, and these usually ask for English at B2 level or higher. For example, Rīga Stradiņš University offers its medicine program in English.

Tuition fees

According to Study in Latvia, yearly fees for international students start below 1,600 EUR for some bachelor's programs and go up to 15,000 EUR in health and medicine. Rīga Stradiņš University charges a yearly fee of 13,500 EUR for its English-taught medicine program in the 2026/2027 academic year.

Student visa

For study at an accredited higher education institution, you get a residence permit. Before the documents are submitted, the inviting institution must have its invitation approved at the Office of Citizenship and Migration Affairs (PMLP/OCMA). Study in Latvia suggests contacting the international office of the chosen university for the visa process.

Proof of funds

A student must show financial means of 500 EUR a month.

Working while studying

Work permit after graduation

The residence permit is given for the length of study plus 4 months after study ends. Master's and doctoral graduates can also ask for a 9-month extension to look for a job. This request must be made before the current permit ends, and approval is not certain.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is it possible to study medicine in English in Latvia?

Yes. Rīga Stradiņš University gives English-taught medicine as a 6-year program, and the 2026/2027 yearly tuition is €13,500. A one-time registration fee of €1,800 is also paid at the start. For the September 2027 term, applicants who need a visa apply between 1 March and 1 May 2027.

How do you get a student visa and residence permit?

For full-time study you need a residence permit, and first the university has your invitation approved at PMLP. Turkish citizens apply to VFS Global for a 90-day multiple-entry D visa. After you enter Latvia, you apply for the residence permit at PMLP.

Which English level is asked for?

English-taught programs usually ask for at least B2. Riga Business School defines this as 6.0 in IELTS and also holds a math exam. The University of Latvia asks for proof of proficiency from applicants who did not study in the English-speaking countries on its list.
