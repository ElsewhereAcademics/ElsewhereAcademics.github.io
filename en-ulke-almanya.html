Germany

At most public universities in Germany there is no tuition fee for a bachelor's degree. Students pay a semester contribution each term (for example €402 in Hamburg for winter 2026/27). Baden-Württemberg, however, charges students from outside the EU a fee of €1,500 per semester. A student whose diploma is not enough for direct entry prepares with the Studienkolleg, which usually lasts two semesters.

GERMANY

Studying in Germany

01

Which route does a Turkish high school diploma open?

In their note dated 30 July 2024, Germany's missions in Türkiye set two conditions for direct entry. You need to score above 180 in YKS (Türkiye's university entrance exam) and be placed in a program. A student who does not meet these conditions usually prepares with a Studienkolleg. The Studienkolleg mostly lasts two semesters and ends with the Feststellungsprüfung exam.

02

How does a study year work?

The year is split into two semesters. The winter semester runs from mid-October to the end of February, and the summer semester from mid-April to the end of July. For example, classes for the 2027/28 winter semester at Heidelberg start on 11 October 2027. A bachelor's degree at universities usually takes six semesters, so three years.

03

Language exam for German-taught programs

Programs taught in German ask for enough knowledge of German. According to the note from the missions in Türkiye, you show this with a language exam at the university before classes start. This exam can be repeated only once. If the exam is not passed, the provisional admission also falls away.

04

Working while studying and the monthly money requirement

Students from outside the EU can work 140 full days or 280 half days a year. Compulsory internships of the program do not count toward this limit. For the visa you show at least €992 a month, so €11,904 a year. This is usually done with a blocked account (Sperrkonto).

05

After graduation

A graduate from outside the EU who finishes their studies in Germany can get a residence permit of up to 18 months to look for a qualified job. During this time they can do any kind of work. The permit cannot be extended after this period.

GERMANY

Key points for Germany

01

No tuition fee, but a semester contribution

At public universities there is generally no tuition fee for a bachelor's degree and most master's degrees. A semester contribution is paid each term (for example €402 in Hamburg for winter 2026/27), and it often includes a public transport ticket. Baden-Württemberg charges students from outside the EU a fee of €1,500 per semester. In Bavaria, universities can set their own fees for students from outside the EU.

02

Blocked account: €11,904 a year

For a student visa you show at least €992 a month, so €11,904 a year. This is usually done with a blocked account called a Sperrkonto, and the money is released to the student at 1/12 each month. The amount is tied to BAföG and may change.

IN BRIEF

Germany in brief

Bachelor'sMaster'sGeneral

Last checked: October 2026. Rules and amounts can change. We check the current situation together before you apply.

Bachelor's: length and application route

A bachelor's degree lasts six, seven or eight semesters. It is usually six semesters at universities, and six or seven semesters including the internship semester at universities of applied sciences. A foreign high school diploma must give the right of entry to higher education in the student's own country. Applicants from some countries go through the Studienkolleg or an assessment exam.

Master's: length and application route

A master's degree lasts two, three or four semesters.

Language of instruction

To be admitted to bachelor's programs taught in German, applicants must show enough knowledge of German.

Tuition fees

At public universities, most states charge no tuition fee for a bachelor's degree. Students pay a semester contribution each term. In Baden-Württemberg, students from outside the EU pay a fee of €1,500 per semester. In Bavaria, universities can set fees for students from outside the EU.

Student visa

Study in Germany is done with a residence title for study purposes issued under Section 16b of the Residence Act. Applicants get a student visa before entering the country.

Proof of funds

According to the amount published by German missions abroad, a student must show at least €992 a month, so €11,904 a year. This is usually done with a blocked account (Sperrkonto). DAAD gives this amount as of 1 January 2025.

Working while studying

Students from outside the EU can work 140 full days or 280 half days a year. For student assistant jobs inside the university there is no day limit, but they must be reported to the foreigners' office.

Work permit after graduation

Graduates from outside the EU who complete their studies in Germany can get a residence permit of up to 18 months to look for a qualified job. During this time they can do any kind of work. The permit cannot be extended.

Academic calendar

The winter semester runs from mid-October to the end of February, and the summer semester from mid-April to the end of July.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is it possible to study at a high school in Germany?

Yes, it is possible with a residence permit for school attendance. This permit is normally issued from 9th grade. As a rule, the student stays in the boarding section (Internat) of the school. For students under 18, notarized consent from the parents is needed.

Is there really no tuition fee at a public university?

In most states there is no tuition fee for bachelor's degrees and most master's degrees, but a semester contribution is paid each term. This contribution often includes a public transport ticket too. In Hamburg for winter 2026/27, €226.80 of the €402 is for the ticket. Baden-Württemberg and TUM, however, charge fees to students from outside the EU.

Is health insurance compulsory in Germany?

Yes, everyone in Germany must have health insurance, and proof of insurance is asked for at university enrollment. Students under 30 can use the student rate in public insurance. Because public insurance starts after enrollment, private insurance is needed until that date.

What are the first things to do after arriving in Germany?

You report your new address to the local registration office within two weeks. Then you apply to the foreigners' office for a residence permit. This is done while the visa is valid and within 90 days of entry. The card can take a few months to arrive.

When should we start looking for housing?

DAAD recommends starting to look for housing and applying for a student residence about three months before classes begin. For a student starting in early October, that is July. The accommodation for the first nights should be arranged about two months before you leave.
