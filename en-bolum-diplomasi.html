Diplomacy

Diplomacy

Diplomacy covers representing a country's interests abroad and supporting its citizens and businesses. It is mostly studied at master's level after an international relations degree.

Law and politics

What do graduates do?

  • Foreign service and diplomatic roles
  • International organisations
GOOD TO KNOW

Before you apply

Each country has its own exam and citizenship rule for diplomats; in the UK, for example, British citizenship is required. Studying diplomacy abroad builds knowledge but does not by itself qualify you as a diplomat. See also our International Relations page.
