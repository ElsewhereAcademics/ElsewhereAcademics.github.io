Spain

A bachelor's in Spain is 240 ECTS and takes four years for a full-time student. A student who finishes high school outside the EU has the diploma recognized as equivalent to the Spanish Bachillerato and gets an admission document from UNEDasiss. Under the new regulation that came into force in May 2025, students can work up to 30 hours a week.

SPAIN

Studying in Spain

01

The route to a public university: recognition (denklik) and UNEDasiss

A student who finishes high school in Türkiye first has the diploma recognized as equivalent to the Spanish Bachillerato (homologación). Then UNEDasiss issues a document showing the admission grade. With high school grades only, this grade can reach at most 6 points. A student who wants to raise the grade takes the PCE exams, and the admission grade is then calculated out of 14.

source · source
02

Private universities have their own application rounds

Private universities such as IE University take students in their own rounds. At IE, round 1 closes on 6 November 2026, round 2 on 15 January 2027 and round 3 on 5 March 2027. The 2027/28 annual fee for IE's Applied Mathematics bachelor's is €27,300.

source · source
03

Four years, two semesters starting in September

A bachelor's at public universities is 240 ECTS, and 60 credits are taken per year, which means four years for a full-time student. The school year starts in September. The first semester ends in December or January, and the second in May or June.

source · source
04

Working 30 hours a week while studying

Under the Foreigners Regulation that came into force in May 2025, students can work up to 30 hours a week. A higher education student does not need a separate work permit for this. Working hours must not clash with classes.

source · source
05

24 months after graduation

A student who completes a program in Spain at bachelor's level or above can get a 24-month residence permit to look for work or start a business. This permit cannot be extended. The application is made within the 60 days before the study permit ends.

source

SPAIN

Key points for Spain

01

24-month job search permit after graduation

Those who study at an authorized institution in Spain at bachelor's level or above can get a 24-month residence permit to look for work or start a business. This permit cannot be extended. The application is made within the 60 days before the study permit ends.

IN BRIEF

Spain in brief

Bachelor'sGeneral

Last checked: October 2026. Rules and amounts can change. We check the current situation together before you apply.

Bachelor's: length and application route

Official university bachelor's programs are 240 ECTS credits, organized at 60 credits per year, which equals four years in full-time study. A student applying from Türkiye must first get recognition (denklik) of the high school diploma as equivalent to the Spanish bachillerato (homologación).

Student visa

For study longer than 90 days, you get a long-stay student visa (visado de estudios) from Spanish consulates.

Proof of funds

The student must show funds equal to 100% of IPREM for each month of stay. According to a January 2025 document from the Consulate in Casablanca, this amount was €600 a month (€7,200 a year) for 2024.

Working while studying

Under the regulation that came into force in May 2025, students can work up to 30 hours a week.

Work permit after graduation

Those who complete their studies at an authorized higher education institution in Spain at bachelor's level or above can get a 24-month residence permit that cannot be extended (Autorización de residencia para búsqueda de empleo o emprender un proyecto empresarial) to look for work or start a business. The application is made within the 60 days before the study permit ends.

Academic calendar

The academic year starts in September. The first semester runs from September to December or January, and the second from January or February to May or June.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much money must show in the account for the visa?

Funds equal to 100% of IPREM are asked for each month of stay. IPREM is €600 a month in 2026, which comes to €5,400 for a nine-month school year. We suggest checking the consulate's current list before you apply.

source · source
At a public university, does a Turkish student pay the same fee as a Spanish student?

It varies by region. In Madrid, a non-EU student without residence status pays the fourth enrollment rate, and a student stay permit does not count as residence. In Asturias, the Universidad de Oviedo applies the third enrollment rate for 2026/27, and in Catalonia each university sets the amount itself.

source · source
Is it required to take the PCE exams?

It is not required, but with high school grades only, the UNEDasiss admission grade reaches at most 6 points. With the PCE, the grade is calculated out of 14. In 2026 the PCE was held in Spain from 25 to 29 May and from 2 to 8 September.

source · source
Which exam for proof of Spanish, and when?

The Instituto Cervantes DELE exam is held in February, April, May, July, September, October and November in 2027. The sessions that offer all levels are 22 May and 13 November 2027. Because not every center opens every session, we suggest asking the centers in Istanbul and Ankara separately.

source
How much does a student spend per month in Barcelona?

Universitat Pompeu Fabra shows a total of €800 to €950 for a student renting a room in a shared flat. Of this, €350 to €500 is rent, €200 is food and €50 is transport. In a student residence the total goes up to €950 to €1,250.

source · source
