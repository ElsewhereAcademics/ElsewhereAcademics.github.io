Linguistics and Modern Languages

Linguistics and Modern Languages

Linguistics studies the scientific structure, function and historical development of language. In modern languages programs, the grammar of one or more foreign languages, translation and spoken communication skills are studied together with the literature and culture of the societies that speak the language.

Example courses

Introduction to linguisticsWritten and spoken grammarTranslation and interpretingHistory of languageLiterature and culture

What do graduates do?

  • Translator
  • Interpreter
  • English as a foreign language teacher
  • Lexicographer
  • Editor and proofreader
  • Speech and language therapist
  • Diplomat
  • Public relations specialist
IN HIGH SCHOOL

Which subjects matter?

For its linguistics bachelor's (BA), UCL asks for AAB at A-level. There is no required subject, but it prefers English Language. In the IB, it also prefers English Language among the Higher Level subjects.

source

Example: University College London, first-year courses

  1. Introduction To Generative Grammar
  2. Core Issues in Linguistics
  3. Introduction to Childrens Language Development
  4. Introduction to Semantics and Pragmatics A
  5. Introduction to Phonetics and Phonology A
  6. Introduction to Phonology

Length by country

United Kingdom
UCL Linguistics BA 3 years.
USA
A bachelor's degree is usually completed in 4 years of full-time study.
Netherlands
A bachelor's at a research university (WO) takes 3 years, 180 ECTS. At a university of applied sciences (HBO) it takes 4 years, 240 ECTS.
Germany
At a university, a bachelor's degree takes 3 years, 180 ECTS. At a Fachhochschule it usually takes 3 or 3.5 years, 180 or 210 ECTS.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is there a linguistics bachelor's in English in Europe?

Yes. Leiden University's linguistics bachelor's is in English, is taught in Leiden and takes 3 years. The program includes courses that introduce a new language such as Hittite or Leti. The school also has a linguistics option taught in Dutch.

source
Is it possible to spend a year abroad while studying linguistics?

In some programs, yes. At UCL, a separate program, Linguistics (International Programme) BA, includes a year abroad. The standard Linguistics BA page does not mention a year abroad.

source
