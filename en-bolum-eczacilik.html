Pharmacy

Pharmacy

Pharmacy teaches the discovery and preparation of medicines and their safe and effective use in treating diseases. Besides courses based on chemistry and biology, the program includes clinical pharmacology, preparing medicines and professional ethics.

Example courses

Pharmaceutical chemistryDrug discovery and drug delivery systemsClinical pharmacologyMicrobiologyPharmacy law and ethics

What do graduates do?

  • Community pharmacist
  • Hospital pharmacist
  • Pharmacologist
  • Toxicologist
  • Clinical research specialist
  • Regulatory affairs specialist
  • Pharmaceutical product development scientist
  • Medical sales representative
IN HIGH SCHOOL

Which subjects matter?

For pharmacy (MPharm), the University of Portsmouth asks for chemistry at A-level. The second subject must be a science subject or mathematics. For 2027 entry, the offer is between ABB and BBB.

source

Example: University College London, first-year courses

  1. Role of the Pharmacist in Health Care
  2. Chemistry of Medicines
  3. Body Systems and Therapeutics 1: Underpinning Principles of Cell and System Function and Drug Action
  4. Making Safe and Effective Medicines
  5. Clinical Professional and Scientific Skills 1

Length by country

United Kingdom
UCL Pharmacy MPharm 4 years. After graduation come 1 year of foundation training and the GPhC registration exam.
Germany
In Germany, this field is one of the programs that end with a Staatsprüfung (state exam). The length varies by program, and Nuffic does not give a number of years.

Exams required for this field

Interview and test United Kingdom

UCL Pharmacy MPharm does not name a specific exam. Interviews and tests are usually held between December and April.

Semmelweis entrance exam Hungary

At Semmelweis, pharmacy applicants take the same exam as medicine and dentistry: tests in biology, chemistry, and general and medical English, followed by an interview.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does a student studying pharmacy abroad face the YKS requirement for equivalence (denklik)?

For students who finished high school in Türkiye, this depends on the university's ranking. If the university is not in the top 1,000 of at least two of the THE, QS, ARWU and CWTS Leiden lists, a YKS requirement applies. In that case, you need to have reached the band required for pharmacy in the YKS of the year you started your studies.

source · source
Does a pharmacy graduate take an exam for equivalence (denklik)?

If YÖK sees gaps in the core learning outcomes of the degree, it can decide on a level assessment exam (STS). ÖSYM holds the pharmacy STS once a year. To pass, you need at least 40 out of 100.

source
Is a bachelor's degree enough to become a pharmacist in the United Kingdom?

No, there are three steps. First you get a GPhC-approved MPharm degree, then you do 52 weeks of foundation training. In the last step, you pass the GPhC registration exam and register as a pharmacist.

source · source
