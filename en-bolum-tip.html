Medicine

Medicine

Medical education studies the structure and function of the human body, and the causes, diagnosis and treatment of diseases. Alongside theory classes, clinical practice and communication with patients are an important part of the training.

Example courses

AnatomyBiochemistryBody systemsEpidemiologyDiagnosis, clinical tests and patient communication

What do graduates do?

  • General practitioner
  • Hospital doctor
  • Specialist doctor (such as surgeon, cardiologist, psychiatrist)
  • Pathologist
  • Epidemiologist
  • Academic and medical researcher
  • Healthcare manager
  • Science writer
IN HIGH SCHOOL

Which subjects matter?

University College London asks for chemistry and biology at A-level for medicine (MBBS). The standard offer for 2027 entry is A*AA; an A* and an A are expected in these two subjects. In the IB, chemistry and biology must be at Higher Level, with a total score of 39.

source

  1. Foundations of Health and Medical Practice
  2. Infection and Defence

Length by country

United Kingdom
UCL Medicine MBBS BSc takes 6 years; a BSc year is included in between. People who already have a bachelor's degree in the United Kingdom can finish in 5 years.
Hungary
The Semmelweis medicine program in English takes 6 years, 12 semesters.
USA
Medical school usually takes 4 years. You need to complete the prerequisite courses (pre-med) before applying.
Germany
In Germany, this field is one of the programs that end with a Staatsprüfung (state exam). The length varies by program, and Nuffic does not give a number of years.

Exams required for this field

UCAT United Kingdom

UCL Medicine applicants take the UCAT in the year they apply. Cambridge Medicine (A100) also asks for the UCAT; pre-registration is required.

MCAT USA

Applicants to medical school in the US must take the MCAT. The applicant is expected to complete the prerequisite courses at a four-year institution first.

IMAT Italy

The entrance exam for English-taught medicine and dentistry programs at public universities. The 2026/27 exam was held on 29 September 2026: 60 questions, 100 minutes, registration through Universitaly.

MedAT-H Austria

The joint exam of the medical universities of Vienna, Innsbruck and Graz, and JKU Linz. In 2026, 16,880 people applied for 1,950 places. At least 95% of the medical places are reserved for EU citizens.

Toelatingsexamen arts Belgium (Flemish region)

To start medicine at Flemish universities you need to pass the entrance exam and rank well. The 2027 exam is on 2 July, and registration runs from 1 March to 17 May 2027. The exam is on paper.

Semmelweis entrance exam Hungary

Tests in medical biology, medical chemistry, general English and medical English (20 questions and 20 minutes each), followed by an interview. The exams are held from early February to the end of June.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do students who study medicine abroad face the YKS (Türkiye's university entrance exam) requirement for recognition (denklik) in Türkiye?

For a student who finished high school in Türkiye, this depends on the university's ranking. If the university is not in the top 1,000 in at least two of the THE, QS, ARWU and CWTS Leiden lists, a YKS requirement applies. In that case, the student needs to have reached the success bracket required for medicine in the YKS of the year they started the program.

source · source
Can a medicine graduate be asked to take a level assessment exam during the recognition (denklik) process?

If YÖK (Türkiye's Council of Higher Education) sees gaps in basic competencies in the diploma, it can decide on a Level Assessment Exam (STS). ÖSYM holds the Medical Doctor STS twice a year; the pass mark is 40 out of 100. For people who get an STS Clinical Sciences decision, results from USMLE Step 3, PLAB Part 2, AMC Clinical, MCCQE Part 2 or the 3rd Staatsexamen can also be accepted.

source · source
If gaps are found in recognition (denklik), do you have to do clinical practice?

In the subjects where it sees gaps, YÖK can decide on things such as completing courses, a level assessment exam, clinical practice or completing an internship. Clinical practice is done under the supervision of the relevant specialist doctors. Its aim is to ensure adaptation to the Turkish health system and to complete the clinical practice that was found missing.

source
