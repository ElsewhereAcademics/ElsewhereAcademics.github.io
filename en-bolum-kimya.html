Chemistry

Chemistry

Chemistry studies the structure, properties and reactions of matter. Students build laboratory skills and learn about areas such as physical chemistry, solid state chemistry, medicinal and biological chemistry, and computational chemistry.

Natural sciences

Chemistry laboratory skillsPhysical chemistrySolid state chemistryMedicinal and biological chemistryComputational chemistry

What do graduates do?

  • Analytical chemist
  • Research scientist (physical sciences)
  • Medicinal chemist
  • Food technologist
  • Forensic scientist
  • Toxicologist
  • Laboratory technician
  • Data analyst
IN HIGH SCHOOL

Which subjects matter?

Imperial College London asks for an A in chemistry and maths at A-level for chemistry (MSci Chemistry with a Year in Industry). For the third subject, biology, economics or physics is preferred (2027 entry).

Example: University College London (UCL), first-year courses

  2. Chemical Skills

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What changes if a chemistry program is accredited by a professional body?

Imperial College London's MSci Chemistry with a Year in Industry is accredited by the Royal Society of Chemistry (RSC). This program fully meets the academic requirements for the Chartered Chemist (CChem) title.

Ask your question on WhatsApp
Can you do a year in industry during a chemistry bachelor's?

In some programs, yes. In Imperial College London's 5-year MSci Chemistry with a Year in Industry, the 4th year is spent in industry. The school describes this year as paid industry experience.

Ask your question on WhatsApp
Is there a YKS requirement for recognition (denklik) of a chemistry degree earned abroad?

YÖK's YKS ranking requirement applies only to medicine, dentistry, pharmacy and law degrees. People who study other programs, such as chemistry, are exempt. For the degree you still need recognition and denklik from YÖK; the preliminary application is made through e-Devlet or the YÖK website.
