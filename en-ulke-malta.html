Malta

English language schools in Malta are licensed by the ELT Council. The list published in 2026 has 33 licensed schools. The schools are in places like Sliema, St. Julian's, St. Paul's Bay and Valletta, and there is one school on the island of Gozo. A non-EU student enrolled in a course longer than 90 days can work up to 20 hours a week with a Jobsplus permit.

MALTA

Studying in Malta

01

English is an official language here

According to Article 5 of the Constitution of Malta, the national language is Maltese. Maltese and English are the two official languages of the country. You can write to government offices in either language and get the reply in the same language.

source
02

Language schools are licensed by the ELT Council

To open a school that teaches English to foreigners in Malta, you need an ELT Council license. The license is based on the regulation L.N. 221 of 2015 and is valid for 4 years. Teachers who teach at these schools also need ELT Council permission. The Council's current list has 33 licensed schools. One of them is on the island of Gozo, in Kercem.

source · source
03

Visa for Turkish students

VFS Global receives Malta's visa applications in Türkiye. Summer schools are also listed among the purposes of a short-stay visa. For courses of up to 90 days, Identità issues a multiple-entry Schengen C visa and adds 7 days to the length of the course. You can apply at any VFS center in Türkiye, there is no regional limit. You can apply at the earliest 6 months and at the latest 15 days before travel, and processing can take up to 45 days.

source · source
04

A course week: teens and adults

At the three schools we reviewed, the standard General English course is 20 lessons a week. At BELS and IH Malta this comes to 15 hours. In Maltalingua's program for ages 8 to 17, classes average 15 students, with a maximum of 20. Maltalingua's summer packages include accommodation, full board, activities, return airport transfer and 24/7 supervision. IH Malta asks students aged 12 to 16 to come with a parent or guardian.

source · source

MALTA

Important for Malta

01

List of licensed schools and courses for teens

The ELT Council's 2026 list includes 33 licensed English schools. The only school on Gozo is in Kercem, and the others are on the island of Malta. The list also includes course types for young people, such as General English for Juniors and Intensive English for Teens.

02

Work permit after day 91

A non-EU language student can apply for a work permit if the course is longer than 90 days. You can start work at the earliest on your 91st day in Malta, and only after the Jobsplus permit is issued. You can work up to 20 hours a week.

Working while studying

Non-EU students on English courses at ELT Council licensed institutions can work up to 20 hours a week if their courses are longer than 90 days. A work permit issued by Jobsplus is needed before starting work.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much is the visa fee, and where do we apply?

According to VFS Global's Türkiye page, the short-stay visa is 90 euros for ages 12 and over and 45 euros for ages 6 to 12. Under age 6 there is no fee. A 30 euro service fee is added to each application, and payment is in cash euros only. The centers are in Ankara, Istanbul (Harbiye and Altunizade), Izmir, Bursa, Gaziantep, Edirne, Antalya and Trabzon.

source · source
How can we check that a school is licensed?

You can look for the school's name in the list on the ELT Council website. The list also gives each school's address and website. Home tutors and distance learning providers are under a separate heading. For example, EC Malta, International House Malta and BELS Malta are on this list.

source · source
Can a student under 18 go to Malta alone?

It depends on the school. Under Malta's rules, the school must provide a student under 18 with supervision suitable for their age and an emergency contact line that can be reached 24 hours a day. Maltalingua's packages for ages 8 to 17 are offered with 24/7 supervision. IH Malta asks students aged 12 to 16 to come with a parent or guardian.

source · source
Is it possible to work during a summer course?

No. A language student can apply for a work permit only if the course is longer than 90 days. Jobsplus issues the permit, and it allows up to 20 hours a week. The student can start work at the earliest on their 91st day in Malta.

source · source
What changes if the course is longer than 3 months?

A student who will stay more than 105 days must get a long-stay student visa through VFS Global before coming to Malta. On VFS's Türkiye page, this D visa costs 150 euros. Identità asks you to show money equal to at least 75% of the national minimum wage for each month of study.

source · source
