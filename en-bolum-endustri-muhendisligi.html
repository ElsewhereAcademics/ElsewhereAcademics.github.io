Industrial Engineering

Industrial Engineering

Industrial engineering deals with analyzing, developing and improving integrated systems that run production and business processes. Optimization, probability models, simulation, quality control and production planning are the main subjects.

Example courses

Deterministic models and optimizationStochastic processesSimulation modelingFacility planning and designProduction planning and control

What do graduates do?

  • Industrial engineer
  • Process engineer
  • Quality engineer
  • Continuous improvement engineer
  • Production engineer
  • Operations manager
  • Supply chain analyst
  • Project manager
IN HIGH SCHOOL

Which subjects matter?

For its Industrial Engineering and Management bachelor's, the University of Twente asks for a good level of Advanced Mathematics. Your high school diploma must also be considered equivalent to a Dutch VWO diploma.

Example: University of Twente, first-year courses

  1. Module 1: Intro to Industrial Engineering & Management
  2. Module 2: Operations management
  3. Module 3: Business and Process Analytics
  4. Module 4: Supply chain management

Length by country

Netherlands
University of Twente Industrial Engineering & Management bachelor's takes 3 years, with 4 modules each year and 15 EC per module.
USA
A bachelor's degree is usually completed in 4 years of full-time study.
Germany
At a university, a bachelor's degree takes 3 years, 180 ECTS. At a Fachhochschule it usually takes 3 or 3.5 years, 180 or 210 ECTS.

Exams required for this field

TOL, TOLC-I, CEnT-S or SAT Italy

For Politecnico di Milano engineering bachelor's degrees, one of these exams is required for 2026/27. The page also mentions the BSc Industrial Engineering program in Piacenza.

TOLC-I Italy

Some Italian universities ask for the TOLC-I for entry to engineering bachelor's degrees. The mathematics section has 20 questions and lasts 50 minutes.

One school from each country

No QS subject ranking is published for this field. The school list is drawn up together at the first meeting, based on the student's goal.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can you study industrial engineering in English in Europe?

Yes, there are examples. The University of Twente's Industrial Engineering and Management bachelor's is fully in English. The Industrial Engineering bachelor's at Politecnico di Milano's Piacenza campus is also in English and takes 3 years. It covers operations, logistics and automation courses.

Why does accreditation matter when choosing an industrial engineering program?

The Washington Accord is an agreement between the bodies that accredit engineering bachelor's programs. ABET in the US, the Engineering Council in the United Kingdom, Engineers Ireland in Ireland and MÜDEK in Türkiye are part of this agreement. Member bodies treat the programs accredited by one another as equivalent.

Is an industrial engineering degree from abroad recognized in Türkiye?

To use it in Türkiye, you get recognition and equivalence (denklik) from YÖK (Türkiye's Council of Higher Education). Equivalence means your degree is found equal to its counterpart in Türkiye. The preliminary application is made through e-Devlet or the YÖK website.
