Studying in Sweden

01

Apply through one portal: universityadmissions.se

Bachelor's and master's applications in Sweden are made through a single portal, universityadmissions.se. For autumn 2027 entry, applications opened on 16 October 2026 and the final day is 15 January 2027. Documents and, for non-EU applicants, the 900 SEK application fee must be completed by 1 February 2027.

02

Bachelor's selection: diploma grade and, at some schools, an exam

The International Business bachelor's at Lund University is 180 credits and three years. In the international early round, selection is made only by high school grade average. Stockholm School of Economics asks for an SAT, ACT or ITB-Business result and adds a second application step.

03

Tuition: 80,000 to 295,000 SEK a year

According to Study in Sweden, the tuition for one academic year ranges from 80,000 SEK to 295,000 SEK. At Stockholm University, the annual fee is 90,000 SEK in humanities and social sciences and 140,000 SEK in natural sciences. Tuition is paid in advance at the start of the semester, and KTH issues a separate invoice for each semester.

04

Working while studying: 15 hours a week during term time

For study permits granted on or after 11 June 2026, you can work up to 15 hours a week during term time. This limit does not apply in June, July and August. Before, there was no hour limit for students in Sweden.

05

A one-year job search permit after graduation

Those who finish a bachelor's or master's program can get a residence permit of up to one year to look for work. You apply for this permit before the current study permit ends.

Key points for Sweden

01

Autumn 2027 calendar

According to Stockholm University's calendar, the first application round for the autumn 2027 semester opened on 16 October 2026. The final application date is midnight (CET) on 15 January 2027, and the deadline for supporting documents is 1 February. Results are announced on 1 April for master's and on 8 April for bachelor's.

IN BRIEF

Sweden in brief

Bachelor'sMaster'sGeneral

Last checked: October 2026. Rules and amounts can change. We check the current situation together before you apply.

Bachelor's: length and application route

Bachelor's applications are made through the central portal universityadmissions.se, and applications for the autumn semester usually close in mid-January. Applicants from outside the EU/EEA and Switzerland pay an application fee of 900 SEK. According to Stockholm University, the final application date for autumn 2027 is 15 January 2027 and the deadline for documents is 1 February 2027.

Master's: length and application route

Master's applications are also made through universityadmissions.se. Program lengths vary by institution. For example, all master's programs at Umeå Institute of Design last two years.

Tuition fees

Students from outside the EU/EEA and Switzerland pay an application fee and tuition. According to Study in Sweden, the tuition for one academic year is between about 80,000 and 295,000 SEK, with an average of about 129,000 SEK. For example, Karlstad University gives total program fees for 2026/2027 as 285,000 to 450,000 SEK for bachelor's and 200,000 to 580,000 SEK for master's.

Student visa

The document needed for study is called 'Residence permit for studies', and the application can be made through Migrationsverket's online application system. Migrationsverket calls this permit 'residence permit for first or second cycle studies' for bachelor's and master's.

Proof of funds

For applications made in 2026, at least 10,656 SEK a month in living funds must be shown. For 2025 applications this amount was 10,584 SEK.

Working while studying

For study permits granted on or after 11 June 2026, bachelor's and master's students can work up to 15 hours a week during term time. This limit does not apply in June, July and August. Work linked to the school or program, such as internships, student representation and research, is also an exception.

Work permit after graduation

Those who complete a bachelor's or master's program can get a residence permit of up to one year to look for work. The application must be made before the current study permit ends.

Academic calendar

Most English-language programs start in the autumn semester. For programs starting in the spring semester there is a separate application period in mid-August.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

When should we apply for the residence permit?

Migrationsverket recommends that you apply as soon as the acceptance letter arrives. If tuition is to be paid, tuition is paid first and then the application is made. The adult application fee is 1,500 SEK, and 75% of recent decisions were given within 3 months.

When do we pay tuition, and what happens if we are late?

At Lund University, invoices for autumn entry are sent between the end of March and April, and the payment deadline is 20 May. If this date is missed, the place on the program is lost. Paying early also lets you apply for the residence permit on time.

How much money must we show in the bank account?

Migrationsverket asks for at least 10,656 SEK a month in living funds for applications made in 2026. This amount is separate from tuition, and tuition must be paid before the permit application. The figure may change when the new amount is announced for 2027 applications.

If we miss the January deadline, is there a second chance?

A second round opens on universityadmissions.se in March 2027, and results are announced on 9 July 2027. In this round, the time left for tuition, the residence permit and travel becomes very short.

Can a high school student take a course in Sweden in summer?

SSHL, a boarding school in Sigtuna, runs a Swedish intensive summer course for students aged 12 to 16. The 2027 course runs from 18 to 30 July and the total fee is 39,900 SEK. The fee includes classes, room, meals and activities.
