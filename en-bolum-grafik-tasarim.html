Graphic Design

Graphic Design

Graphic design teaches how to communicate ideas and messages visually and effectively. Typography, publication design, illustration, user interface and experience design, and motion graphics are the main subjects.

Example courses

Typography and publication designUser interface (UI) and interaction designUser experience (UX) designMotion graphics and animationPackaging design

What do graduates do?

  • Graphic designer
  • Advertising art director
  • Illustrator
  • UX designer
  • Web designer
  • Animator
  • Creative director
  • Digital marketing specialist
IN HIGH SCHOOL

Which subjects matter?

The University of Edinburgh does not ask for a specific A-level or IB subject for graphic design. Most applicants have ABB, or 34 points in the IB. A digital portfolio showing artistic skill is also required.

Example: University of the Arts London (UAL), first-year courses

  1. Unit 1: Introduction to Graphic Design
  2. Unit 2: Type
  3. Unit 3: Image
  4. Unit 4: Publish

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is asked for in a graphic design portfolio?

For 2026 entry, the University of Edinburgh asked for a single PDF of up to 20 pages. Up to 5 pages are given to each of the four assessment criteria. UAL Camberwell asks for up to 30 pages and expects research and process work to be included too.

Is a Foundation Diploma compulsory for graphic design?

It is not compulsory for UAL Camberwell's graphic design bachelor's. It appears as one of the accepted qualifications. UAL's one-year Foundation Diploma in Art and Design is also open to international students and prepares them for art and design bachelor's degrees.
