Austria

The academic year consists of the winter semester starting on 1 October and the summer semester starting on 1 March.

AUSTRIA

Studying in Austria

01

How does a Turkish high school diploma open the door?

According to the Austrian Ministry of Education's recommendation for Türkiye, a high school diploma alone does not give the right to enter a university. You are asked for at least 180 points in YKS and a document showing that you were placed in a program. The YKS result is valid for five years. Each university makes the admission decision itself.

02

German requirement and the preparation route (VWU)

To apply to German-taught programs at the Universität Wien, an A2 certificate is enough, but C1 is needed to start classes. A student without C1 first enrolls in the preparatory program of the Vienna universities (VWU). Besides German, an additional exam in math, English or history/geography may also be asked for here.

03

A study year and the fee at public universities

The winter semester starts on 1 October and the summer semester on 1 March. At public universities, a student from outside the EU pays a fee of €726.72 per semester. At universities of applied sciences (Fachhochschule), a bachelor's degree is 180 ECTS, so it usually takes three years.

04

Working while studying

With a student residence permit you can work up to 20 hours a week. For this, your employer gets a work permit from AMS before you start work. No labor market test is done for the 20-hour permit.

05

After graduation

A student who graduates in Austria can extend the residence permit once for 12 months to look for work or set up a company. A graduate who receives a job offer matching their profession can move to the Rot-Weiß-Rot Karte.

AUSTRIA

Important for Austria

01

YKS result and placement document

According to the Ministry's October 2025 recommendation for Türkiye, a high school diploma alone does not give the right of entry. You need at least 180 placement points in the university exam and a document showing that you were placed in a program. The YKS result counts as valid for five years; German knowledge is also required. The university's rectorate makes the admission decision.

02

20 hours a week while studying, 12 months after graduation

Students can work up to 20 hours a week with a work permit that the employer obtains from AMS. Graduates can extend their student permit once by 12 months to look for work or start a company. A graduate who gets a suitable job offer can switch to the Rot-Weiß-Rot Karte.

IN BRIEF

Austria in brief

Bachelor'sMaster'sGeneral

Last checked: October 2026. Rules and amounts can change. We check the current situation together before you apply.

Bachelor's: length and application route

At universities of applied sciences and most private institutions, bachelor's programs are 180 ECTS. According to OeAD's note on Türkiye, a Turkish high school diploma is enough if the university entrance exam has also been passed.

Master's: length and application route

Master's programs are 60 or 120 ECTS and last two or four semesters; at public universities they are mostly 120 ECTS.

Tuition fees

Students from outside the EU pay a fee of €726.72 per semester at public universities.

Student visa

A residence permit called 'Aufenthaltsbewilligung Student' is needed for study. The application is usually made at the Austrian representation in the student's country of residence; after a positive decision, a D visa valid for four months is issued for entry to Austria.

Proof of funds

The monthly amount to be shown for 2026 is €722.58 for students under 24 and €1,308.39 for students aged 24 and over.

Working while studying

Students can work up to 20 hours a week with a work permit (Beschäftigungsbewilligung) that the employer obtains from the Arbeitsmarktservice; no labour market test is carried out for this.

Work permit after graduation

People who graduate from a higher education institution in Austria can extend their 'Aufenthaltsbewilligung Student' permit once by 12 months to look for work or start a company. Those who receive a job offer that suits their profession can switch to the Rot-Weiß-Rot Karte for graduates.

Academic calendar

The winter semester starts on 1 October and the summer semester on 1 March.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can a student with weak German apply to Universität Wien?

Yes, an A2 certificate is enough to apply. An admitted student first takes extra exams in the preparatory program of the Viennese universities (VWU) until reaching C1. They start the degree program only after passing all of these exams.

Is it possible to study medicine in Austria?

Medicine and dentistry are entered through the MedAT exam; the expected date for the 2027 exam is 9 July 2027. In 2026/27 there were 1,950 places in the four public medical schools and 16,880 people registered for the exam. At least 95% of the medicine quota is reserved for EU citizens and those treated as equal to them.

How long does the student residence permit take, and where do you apply?

The application is made to the Austrian representation in Türkiye; the Austrian Consulate General handles long-term D visas. If the documents are complete, the decision usually comes within 90 days. OeAD recommends applying at least three months before entry, and six months if possible.

Are the fees at Fachhochschulen and private universities the same as at public ones?

No. Fachhochschulen can normally charge up to €363.36 per semester, but they may ask a non-EU student for a fee that covers the cost. At the private Modul University Vienna, the bachelor's semester fee is €9,000.

How much money do we need to show in the bank?

With the 2026 amounts, a student under 24 shows €722.58 a month and a student aged 24 or over shows €1,308.39 a month. This amount is proven in advance for 12 months; for a student under 24 it comes to about €8,671. The amounts are updated every year.
