EXAM CORNER

Which exams?

The exams most often asked for in applications. Each school decides which exam it asks for and with what score.

Duolingo English Test (DET)Duolingo

Schools and universities accept it as proof of English proficiency; you need to check one by one which schools accept it.

Duration
About 1 hour
Score
From 10 to 160, in steps of 5 points
Validity
2 years
Where
Online, on a computer, at any time and place you like (from home). There is no test center.
Official website
SATCollege Board

Some universities in the US ask for it in applications; whether it is compulsory depends on the school.

Duration
2 hours 14 minutes (Reading and Writing 64, Math 70 minutes) with a 10-minute break in between
Score
Total from 400 to 1600; each of the two sections from 200 to 800
Where
International students take it digitally at a test center.
Fee
$68 registration fee and $43 international fee, $111 in total (valid until June 2027)
Official website
ACTACT

Some universities in the US accept it instead of the SAT; whether it is compulsory depends on the school.

Duration
Compulsory sections take 2 hours 5 minutes (English 35, Math 50, Reading 40 minutes). Optional Science 40 minutes, Writing 40 minutes.
Score
From 1 to 36. The composite score is the average of English, Math and Reading; Science is not included in this score.
Where
Outside the US, it is taken only at a test center, on a computer.
Official website
IELTS AcademicBritish Council, IDP IELTS and Cambridge University Press & Assessment

Asked for as proof of English proficiency in bachelor's and master's applications abroad.

Duration
2 hours 45 minutes (Listening 30, Reading 60, Writing 60, Speaking 11 to 14 minutes). Speaking can be on a different day.
Score
Each section and the overall score are bands from 1 to 9; 0 means the exam was not taken.
Validity
2 years from the exam date
Where
In Türkiye, at test centers on a computer in many cities including Istanbul, Ankara and İzmir. In some countries there is an option to take IELTS Online from home.
Official website
TOEFL iBTETS

Universities abroad ask for it as proof of English proficiency in bachelor's and master's applications.

Duration
About 2 hours. The time can vary slightly because the reading and listening sections are adaptive.
Score
Since 21 January 2026, each section and the total are scored from 1 to 6, in half-point steps. For two years after January 2026, an equivalent score from 0 to 120 is also given.
Validity
2 years from the exam date
Where
On a computer at a test center or at home (TOEFL iBT Home Edition, with a live proctor).
Official website

ELSEWHERECAMPUS FAQ

Frequently asked questions

All questions

Do you need to take the YKS to apply to universities abroad?

For direct entry with a Turkish high school diploma in Germany and Austria, you need at least 180 points in the YKS (Türkiye's university entrance exam) and a place in a program. In Lithuania, Vilnius University asks for a YKS result or at least one year of university documents in addition to the diploma. In Poland, it is enough for the diploma to give the right to apply to a university in Türkiye.

What do you do if a Turkish high school diploma is not accepted directly?

UCL does not accept direct entry to a bachelor's with state, private and Anatolian high school diplomas, and recommends the International Foundation Year. The University of Melbourne and the University of Sydney also point to a foundation program as the way in for diplomas that are not on their list. In Germany this preparation is done through a Studienkolleg; it mostly lasts two semesters and ends with the Feststellungsprüfung exam.

Can we apply before getting the high school diploma?

Yes. In the United Kingdom, a student whose exams are not finished yet gets a conditional offer. The place is confirmed when conditions such as a graduation grade or an English requirement are met. According to the University of Portsmouth, bachelor's applicants meet these conditions in August and September of the start year.

Ask your question on WhatsApp
Can you study a bachelor's in English in countries where English is not the native language?

According to Study in Finland, Finland has about 600 English-taught bachelor's and master's programs; Aalto has six bachelor's programs in English. In 2026 the University of Tartu opened 25 master's programs in English and only 2 bachelor's programs. In Czechia, a student studying in Czech at a public university pays no tuition fee, while English programs charge fees.

Ask your question on WhatsApp
Do you have to choose a major when applying in the US?

No. In the US, a student can enter a university without choosing a major and often makes the decision at the end of the second year. In the first years, you take courses in English, mathematics, science, the humanities and the social sciences. A bachelor's usually takes four years full-time.

JOURNEY

How do we work?

Eight steps, from the first meeting to the first week after the student arrives.

  1. 1

    First meeting

    We listen first. We do not talk about schools before we understand the student's interests, goals and the family's expectations.

  2. 2

    Roadmap

    After setting the goal, we draw the path to it. We share country, field of study and school options with you, together with why we suggest each one.

  3. 3

    Budget plan

    We put the paths that lead to the same goal with different budgets side by side. We make clear from the start which payment is due when.

  4. 4

    Exams and documents

    We build the calendar of exams the schools ask for and prepare the documents step by step.

  5. 5

    Application

    We prepare the file each school asks for in full and complete the applications according to each school's own calendar. We regularly share with you which document was sent to which school and when.

  6. 6

    Decision

    When acceptances arrive, we put the options side by side and make the final decision together, keeping the student's goal in mind.

  7. 7

    Orientation

    Orientation starts with the acceptance. We prepare the student for the new city, the school culture and the first weeks. Depending on your package, visa, accommodation and travel planning are also part of this stage.

  8. 8

    After arrival

    The first mornings in a new city feel a little strange. At the end of the first week, we have a meeting with our student: classes, housing, new friends, suggestions.
