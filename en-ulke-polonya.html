Poland

In Poland, medicine can also be studied as a 6-year English program. For example, the Medical University of Warsaw admits students to its English medicine program with its own competency test. There is no common application system. You apply directly to the admissions office of the university you choose.

POLAND

Studying in Poland

01

Applying with a Turkish high school diploma

Because Türkiye is an OECD member, a Turkish high school diploma is recognized automatically in Poland. The only condition is that the diploma gives the right to apply to university in Türkiye. There is no central placement system. You apply separately to each university's admissions office.

02

Polish or English, how many years?

You can study programs in Polish or English. For English programs, at least B2 level is required. A bachelor's takes 3 to 4 years and a master's takes 1.5 to 2 years. Fields like medicine and dentistry are single-block programs of 4.5 to 6 years.

03

Academic year and tuition examples

The academic year usually starts in early October and has two semesters of 15 weeks each. A foreign student starting the Polish-language economics bachelor's at the University of Warsaw in 2026/27 pays 2,600 euros a year. The same faculty's English Finance, International Investment and Accounting program was 3,550 euros a year in 2025/26, paid in one payment.

04

Working while studying and residence permit

A full-time student with a student visa or temporary residence permit can work without a work permit. A first-year student gets a residence permit for 15 months. In later years the permit is given for the remaining study time plus 3 months, but not for more than 3 years.

05

After graduation

A student who graduates in Poland can get a 9-month temporary residence permit to look for work. This permit is given only once. You apply in person at the voivodeship office while you are legally staying in Poland.

POLAND

Important for Poland

01

An exam or international test score in Warsaw

According to NAWA's program announcement, candidates take the Medical University of Warsaw's competency exam. Alternatively, a BMAT, MCAT, GAMSAT or UKCAT score can be submitted. The program has 3 years of basic medicine and 3 years of clinical training. In the additional intake opened for 2026/2027, online registration closed on 3 October 2026 and the exam was held on 7 October.

02

Medicine tuition is very different from other fields of study

According to Study in Poland, annual tuition averages 2,000 to 2,500 EUR in Polish programs and about 3,000 EUR in English programs. According to NAWA's December 2023 publication, annual tuition in medicine and dentistry is 12,000 to 15,000 EUR and above. Each university sets the exact amount itself.

03

No permit needed while studying, 9 months after graduation

Full-time students with a student visa or temporary residence permit can work without a separate work permit. Graduates can apply for a 9-month temporary residence permit, given only once, to look for work. This application is made in person at the voivodeship office during the legal stay in Poland.

IN BRIEF

Poland at a glance

Language and summer schoolBachelor'sMaster'sGeneral

Last checked: October 2026. Rules and amounts can change. We check the current situation together before you apply.

Language schools and summer schools

One way to prove language proficiency when applying to Polish-taught programs is a nine-month preparatory language course.

Bachelor's: length and application route

A bachelor's (first cycle) takes 3 to 4 years. Fields like medicine, dentistry and law are offered as 4.5 to 6 year integrated master's (long-cycle) programs. To apply, you contact the admissions office of the university you chose. Admission periods usually start in April or May. Dates vary by university: at the Medical University of Warsaw, the last day of online registration for 2026/2027 was 3 October 2026.

Master's: length and application route

A master's (second cycle) takes 1.5 to 2 years. You apply through the admissions office of the university you chose.

Language of instruction

International students can apply to programs taught in Polish or English. Some universities offer programs in other languages too. For English programs, at least B2 level is required.

Tuition fees

In Polish-taught programs annual tuition averages 2,000 to 2,500 EUR, and in English-taught programs about 3,000 EUR. According to NAWA's December 2023 publication, annual tuition in medicine and dentistry is 12,000 to 15,000 EUR and above. Each institution sets the exact amount.

Student visa

For full-time study, a student visa or a temporary residence permit is used. The visa decision and its validity period are decided by the relevant Polish consular authorities. You apply at the Polish embassy or consulate in your country.

Proof of funds

According to the Poznań Voivodeship Office, from 1 January 2025 the threshold for sufficient means of subsistence is 1,010 PLN a month for a one-person household. For people coming from a non-EU country, 2,500 PLN for a return ticket and accommodation costs are added.

Working while studying

Full-time students with a student visa or temporary residence permit can work without a work permit.

Work permit after graduation

Graduates can get a 9-month temporary residence permit, given only once, to look for work. The application is made in person at the voivodeship office right after graduation, while you are legally in Poland.

Academic calendar

The academic year usually starts in early October and has two semesters of 15 weeks each. The spring semester lasts until June.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is it possible to study medicine in Poland, and when do you apply?

Yes, there are English medicine programs. For example, the 2027/28 registration period for English medicine and dentistry at the Medical University of Łódź is from 15 January to 31 October 2027. At the same university, a foreign student studying Polish-language medicine in 2026/27 pays annual tuition of 54,000 zloty.

Where do we apply for the visa, and when?

For long-term study, you apply for the national D visa at VFS Global centers. For the Istanbul consulate the centers are in Harbiye, Altunizade, Izmir, Gaziantep and Trabzon. For the embassy they are in Ankara and Antalya. You apply at the earliest 6 months and at the latest 15 days before travel.

Is studying in English more expensive than studying in Polish?

Usually yes. Study in Poland says English programs average around 3,000 euros a year. At the University of Warsaw economics faculty, the Polish-language bachelor's is 2,600 euros a year for 2026/27. The English finance bachelor's was 3,550 euros a year for 2025/26.

How long is the residence permit given for, and what happens if you change schools?

A first-year student gets a temporary residence permit for 15 months. If you leave school or change university, you must notify the voivodeship within 15 working days, because the basis of the permit no longer exists.

In which cities can you study in Poland?

Study in Poland lists Warsaw, Krakow, Gdańsk, Wrocław, Poznań, Łódź and Katowice as cities students often choose. It also says living in the capital and in tourist cities can be a bit more expensive.
