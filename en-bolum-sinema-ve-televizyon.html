Film and Television Production / Film Studies

Film and television departments teach storytelling for the screen and the technical and creative stages of film and television production. Cinematography, production skills, editing and the relationship between media and society are the main topics.

Example courses

CinematographyStorytelling for the screenProduction skillsAnalogue and digital technologyDigital post production

What do graduates do?

  • Film director
  • Television, film and video producer
  • Film and video editor
  • Camera operator
  • Sound technician
  • Location manager
  • Media researcher
  • Visual effects (VFX) artist
IN HIGH SCHOOL

Which subjects matter?

UAL's Film and Television bachelor's asks for 104 UCAS points from A-levels. English, history, media, business, and art and design are preferred subjects; the University of Edinburgh does not ask for a specific subject.

source · source

Length by country

United Kingdom
UAL LCC BA Film Practice, 3 years; 93 weeks of teaching full-time.
USA
A bachelor's degree is usually completed in 4 years of full-time study.
Netherlands
A bachelor's at a research university takes 3 years (180 ECTS). At an HBO (university of applied sciences) it takes 4 years (240 ECTS).

Exams required for this field

Portfolio and short film United Kingdom

UAL LCC BA Film Practice asks for a three-part portfolio: a film or sound work of up to 3 minutes, a statement of up to 300 words and up to 8 pages of supporting material. Some applicants are invited to an online interview of 15 to 20 minutes.

One school from each country

No QS subject ranking is published for this field. The school list is drawn up together at the first meeting, based on the student's goal.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is a film or portfolio required when applying for a film bachelor's?

At the example schools, yes. UAL London College of Communication's Film Practice bachelor's asks for a film or sound work of up to 3 minutes. It comes with a statement of up to 300 words and 8 pages of supporting material; Edinburgh also asks for a digital portfolio.

source · source
Is there film education in English in Czechia?

At FAMU in Prague, the English option is at master's (MFA) level. Applications for the 2027/2028 directing master's open on 2 November 2026 and close on 21 January 2027. A short film of up to 10 minutes, a 16-frame storyboard and a video interview are required.
