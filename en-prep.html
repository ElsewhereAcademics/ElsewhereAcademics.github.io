BUDGET

What goes into the total budget?

The school fee is not the only cost. We work out all of the items below together in one table, based on the student's school list.

School and boarding fee

At boarding schools, room and board are usually included in the fee. At Australian state schools, homestay is paid separately.

Applications and exams

School application fees, the SSAT and language tests such as TOEFL, IELTS or Duolingo.

Visa

In the USA, the visa application and the I-901 SEVIS fee; in the UK, the Child Student visa; in Canada, the study permit.

Guardianship

In the UK, guardianship is arranged after acceptance; in Canada, the custodianship declaration is prepared with notarisation.

Health insurance

Health insurance with the cover the school or country requires.

Flights

Flights at the start and end of the school year and home for the holidays.

Personal expenses

Uniform, books, school trips and pocket money.

EXAM CORNER

Which exams?

The exams most often asked for in applications. Each school decides which exam it asks for and with what score.

SSAT (Upper Level)E3n (formerly the Enrollment Management Association)

Some private high schools in the US ask for it from students applying to grades 8 to 11.

Duration
3 hours 10 minutes (including two 10-minute breaks)
Sections
The math, reading and verbal sections are scored; the 25-minute writing sample is not scored but is sent to schools.
Validity
Scores from the 2026/27 test year can be sent to schools until 31 July 2028.
Where
Outside the US and Canada, on paper or on a computer at Prometric test centers. Taking the exam at home (SSAT at Home) is available only in the US and Canada.
Official website
TOEFL JuniorETS

Schools use it to see whether the student is ready for class in English.

Duration
The Standard test takes 1 hour 55 minutes (listening 40, language form 25, reading 50 minutes). The separate Speaking test takes 18 minutes and the Writing test about 40 minutes.
Score
In the Standard test, a total from 600 to 900 (each section from 200 to 300). Speaking and Writing from 0 to 16.
Validity
Scores can be reported for 2 years after the exam date.
Where
It is usually taken through the institution that asks for it. The digital version can also be taken at home, under the monitoring rules set by the institution.
Official website
Duolingo English Test (DET)Duolingo

Schools and universities accept it as proof of English proficiency; you need to check one by one which schools accept it.

Duration
About 1 hour
Score
From 10 to 160, in steps of 5 points
Validity
2 years
Where
Online, on a computer, at any time and place you like (from home). There is no test center.
Official website

ELSEWHEREPREP FAQ

Frequently asked questions

All questions

From which grade can a student go to high school abroad?

In the US, students can study at any grade level in SEVP-approved private schools with an F-1 visa. In Germany, a residence permit to attend school is normally given from grade 9. Schools such as Phillips Exeter and Upper Canada College also accept applications from grade 9 applicants.

Ask your question on WhatsApp
How does a one-year exchange program (J-1) in the US differ from F-1?

The J-1 high school exchange program is for students aged 15 to 18.5 when the program starts. The student studies for at least one semester and at most one academic year, and must not have taken part in such a program before. With F-1 at a public high school, the stay cannot exceed 12 months in total; for a longer time, SEVP-approved private schools are chosen.

USA page Ask your question on WhatsApp
Does the student stay in a boarding school or with a family?

J-1 exchange students in the US stay with a host family or in a boarding school. In Germany, a foreign student attending school stays, as a rule, in the boarding section attached to the school (Internat). In Australia, NSW public schools ask for 535 AUD per week for homestay for 2027.

Ask your question on WhatsApp
Is a guardian in that country needed for a student under 18?

In Canada, a student under 17 either comes with a parent or names a custodian who is a Canadian citizen or permanent resident. The custodian and parents declare this arrangement with form IMM 5646, notarized. In Australia, a student under 18 must have an approved welfare arrangement for the visa; the school can provide this with a CAAW document.

Ask your question on WhatsApp
When do boarding school applications close?

In Canada, Ridley College announced 1 December 2026 as the first-round deadline for boarding applicants. Upper Canada College announces the results of grade 9 applications on 26 February 2027. Because the deadline changes from school to school, you need to look at each school's own page.

Is a high school diploma earned abroad valid in Türkiye?

For a high school diploma from abroad, you get recognition (denklik) from the Ministry of National Education (MEB). The application form is filled out through the e-Denklik system. If you are in Türkiye, the original documents are taken to the recognition center. If you are abroad, they are taken to the education counsellor's office or attaché. Graduates are asked for an apostilled or certified copy of the diploma.

Ask your question on WhatsApp
Which grade does a student continue in after spending a year abroad in high school?

On return, a recognition (denklik) application is made to MEB for the year studied abroad. For the in-between grades, the report card, transcript or leaving certificate from the school abroad is asked for. The education system of that country is taken into account, and the grade the student will continue in is set accordingly. If the parent submits a petition, the student can be placed one grade lower. If the documents are not yet available, the student attends school temporarily on a parent's declaration, and the documents are requested within three months.

JOURNEY

How do we work?

Eight steps, from the first meeting to the first week after the student arrives.

  1. 1

    First meeting

    We listen first. We do not talk about schools before we understand the student's interests, goals and the family's expectations.

  2. 2

    Roadmap

    After setting the goal, we draw the path to it. We share country, field of study and school options with you, together with why we suggest each one.

  3. 3

    Budget plan

    We put the paths that lead to the same goal with different budgets side by side. We make clear from the start which payment is due when.

  4. 4

    Exams and documents

    We build the calendar of exams the schools ask for and prepare the documents step by step.

  5. 5

    Applications

    We prepare the file each school asks for in full and complete the applications according to each school's own calendar. We regularly share with you which document was sent to which school and when.

  6. 6

    Decision

    When acceptances arrive, we put the options side by side and make the final decision together, keeping the student's goal in mind.

  7. 7

    Orientation

    Orientation starts with the acceptance. We prepare the student for the new city, the school culture and the first weeks. Depending on your package, visa, accommodation and travel planning are also part of this stage.

  8. 8

    After arrival

    The first mornings in a new city feel a little strange. At the end of the first week, we have a meeting with our student: classes, housing, new friends, suggestions.
