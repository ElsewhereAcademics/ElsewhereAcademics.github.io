Japan

In Japan, the academic year starts in April at most universities. Some schools also take students in September or October. Many universities use the EJU exam for bachelor's applications. The exam is held in June and November. Except for the Japanese language section, the sections can be taken in Japanese or English, and the number of bachelor's programs taught fully in English is growing.

JAPAN

Studying in Japan

01

The exam that opens the door: EJU or direct application?

For programs taught in Japanese, most schools ask for an EJU score. According to JASSO, 479 universities use this score. The EJU is held twice a year, in June and November. Türkiye is not among the 2026 test centers, so the student needs to take the exam in Japan or in another country on the list. English-taught programs such as Keio PEARL and Waseda SILS take applications directly, on their own calendars.

source · source
02

The school year starts in April, English-taught programs in September

At most Japanese universities, the school year starts in April and ends in March of the next year. Some schools also take students in September or October. For example, the enrollment date for Waseda SILS September 2027 entry is 21 September 2027. A bachelor's degree usually takes four years and a master's degree two years.

source · source
03

A bachelor's degree without Japanese

The number of bachelor's programs taught fully in English is growing. The University of Tokyo plans to open its College of Design, with 100 students and all classes in English, in September 2027. The project is still waiting for approval from the Ministry (MEXT). The same university's English-taught PEAK program took its last students in September 2026.

source · source
04

Monthly spending and part-time work

According to JASSO's 2023 survey, an international student who pays for their own studies spends 105,000 yen a month on average. Rent averages 41,000 yen across Japan and 57,000 yen in Tokyo. A student who has permission from the immigration office can work up to 28 hours a week, and up to 8 hours a day during long holidays.

source · source
05

After graduation

A graduate who is looking for a job in Japan can get a six-month residence permit with the recommendation of their school. As a rule, this period can be extended once. When you find a job, you apply to change from Student status to a work status.

source

JAPAN

What matters for Japan

01

EJU: four sections, whichever the school asks for

The EJU is run by JASSO and has four sections: Japanese, Science (physics, chemistry, biology), Japan and the World, and Mathematics. Students take the sections that their target university asks for. The Japanese section is only held in Japanese. The other sections can be taken in Japanese or English. The exam is also held outside Japan.

02

Japanese language school: Ministry of Justice list

To study Japanese with Student status, you need to enroll in a language school that the Minister of Justice has set by official notice. Japanese language schools usually start in April and October, and some start in January or July.

IN BRIEF

Japan in brief

Language and summer schoolBachelor'sMaster'sGeneral

Last checked: October 2026. Rules and amounts can change. We check the current situation together before you apply.

Language schools and summer schools

To study Japanese with Student status, you need to enroll in a language school that the Minister of Justice has set by official notice. Japanese language schools usually start in April and October, and some start in January or July.

Bachelor's: length and application route

A bachelor's degree usually takes four years. Medicine, dentistry, veterinary medicine and some pharmacy programs take six years. You apply to each school separately. Schools may use document review, interviews, the EJU exam or entrance exams made for international students. The EJU is held twice a year, in June and November.

Master's: length and application route

Master's programs normally take two years. Graduate applicants usually need to find an advisor professor for themselves.

Language of instruction

Most classes at Japanese universities are taught in Japanese, and the number of bachelor's programs taught fully in English is growing. For Japanese-taught programs, schools usually ask for JLPT N1 or N2 and at least 250 points in the EJU Japanese section.

Student visa

For study in Japan, the 'Student' residence status is given. This status is recognized only for study purposes.

Working while studying

After getting permission for activities outside the status from the immigration office, students can work up to 28 hours a week, and up to 8 hours a day during long school holidays.

Work permit after graduation

Japan does not have a separate post-graduation work visa. Those who look for a job after graduation can get a six-month residence permit, which as a rule can be extended once, with a recommendation letter from the school. When a job is found, an application is made to change from Student status to a work status.

Academic calendar

At most higher education institutions, the academic year starts in April and ends in March of the next year. Some universities also take students in September or October.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can we take the EJU in Türkiye?

According to the 2026 guide, no. The overseas test centers are in 13 countries and regions, such as India, Korea and Thailand. The Japanese section of the exam is held only in Japanese, and sections such as mathematics and science can also be chosen in English. If you aim for a school that asks for the EJU, plan your travel and dates early.

source · source
Can a student who does not know Japanese study for a bachelor's degree in Japan?

Yes, there are programs taught fully in English. Keio PEARL and Waseda SILS are taking applications for September 2027 entry. The University of Tokyo also plans to open its College of Design, with all classes in English, in September 2027. This program is still waiting for Ministry approval.

source · source
How much money do you need to show in your bank account for a student visa?

We could not find a single amount announced for all of Japan. The school sets the amount. For example, Doshisha University asks for at least 1.2 million yen in living costs on top of one year of school fees. After admission, the school applies to the immigration office for the Certificate of Eligibility. For a student abroad, this usually takes 1 to 2 months.

source · source
How big is the fee difference between a public and a private university?

At Kyoto University, the first year costs 817,800 yen including the enrollment fee (April 2025 figures). At private Keio University's English-taught PEARL program, the 2026 yearly academic fee alone is 1,308,350 yen, and a 200,000 yen entrance fee is added to it.

source · source
Is it possible to go to a language school first and then move to a Japanese-taught field of study?

Yes. To study Japanese with Student status, you need to enroll in a language school announced by the Ministry of Justice. These schools usually start in April and October. For Japanese-taught fields of study, schools mostly ask for JLPT N1 or N2 and at least 250 points in the EJU Japanese section.

source · source
