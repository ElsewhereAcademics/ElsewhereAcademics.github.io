Czechia

In Czechia, students who study in Czech at public universities pay no tuition fee, whatever their nationality. English programs, however, have a fee, and medicine fees are high. For example, for 2025/2026 entry, the First Faculty of Medicine of Charles University held a written exam in biology, chemistry and physics for English medicine and announced an annual fee of €24,250.

CZECHIA

Studying in Czechia

01

No tuition fee for Czech-language study, English programs have a fee

Students who study in Czech at public universities pay no tuition fee, whichever country they come from; only administrative fees such as the application fee are charged. English programs, however, have a fee. For example, the Faculty of Mathematics and Physics of Charles University asks non-EU students for €7,100 a year from 2026/27.

02

Application: each university has its own system and exam

In Czechia there is no single application form for all universities; you apply to each university separately through its own system. The entrance exam also differs by school. The Faculty of Information Technology of Czech Technical University uses the SCIO online exam or its own mathematics exam. The Faculty of Social Studies of Masaryk University holds the online GAP exam for some English programs.

03

What do you spend in a month?

Study in Czechia shows a basic monthly cost of €500 to €800 for a student who lives in a dormitory or a shared flat. A single room in a dormitory costs €250 to €450, and food and drink cost €150 to €230 a month. VŠE in Prague also expects €550 to €800 a month for its own students.

04

Working while studying

A non-EU student who studies full-time in an accredited program can work without getting a separate work permit. The employer reports to the regional branch of the Labour Office that you have started work. According to Study in Czechia, you can expect CZK 150 to CZK 200 an hour in student jobs; the minimum wage is CZK 134.40 an hour as of May 2026.

05

After graduation

A non-EU student who graduates in Czechia can apply for a residence permit valid for up to 9 months to look for work or start a company. This application is made before the current permit ends. Graduates of a Czech university can enter the labour market freely and get an Employee Card in order to work.

For the visa

For a student visa, 15 times the minimum subsistence amount (existenční minimum) is shown for the first month and 2 times for each following month. This amount is currently CZK 3,130 a month. For a 12-month stay it comes to CZK 115,810 in total; ÚJOP of Charles University gives the same amount for the visa.

CZECHIA

Important for Czechia

01

Prague First Faculty of Medicine: written test, then MMI

For 2025/2026 entry, the written exam was a multiple-choice test in biology, chemistry and physics. Then a multiple mini interview (MMI) was held, and only the score from this second stage counted for admission. The application deadline was 30 April, and the written exam was in the week of 9 to 13 June 2025.

IN BRIEF

Czechia in brief

Language and summer schoolBachelor'sMaster'sGeneral

Last checked: October 2026. Rules and amounts can change. We check the current situation together before you apply.

Language schools and summer schools

For those who want to learn Czech, semester, yearly and evening courses are offered in person or online.

Bachelor's: length and application route

Bachelor's programs normally last 3 to 4 years; medicine and veterinary medicine are 6-year integrated programs. There is no single central application form for all universities; you apply separately to each university's own online system, and for most programs starting in September, applications close between February and April. The First Faculty of Medicine of Charles University announced its 2025/2026 annual fee for the English medicine program as €24,250.

Master's: length and application route

Master's programs usually last 1 to 3 years after the bachelor's. Applications are made directly to the institution, through the institution's own system.

Language of instruction

The main language of higher education in Czechia is Czech; many programs are also offered in English and other foreign languages.

Tuition fees

In Czech-language study at public higher education institutions, students of all nationalities pay no tuition fee, apart from administrative fees such as the application fee. Programs taught in a foreign language have a fee; according to DZS's 2023 publication, these fees are mostly between €0 and €6,000 per academic year.

Student visa

The application for a long-term residence permit for study is made in person at the Czech embassy. The decision is given within 60 days of a complete application.

Proof of funds

The required amount is calculated as 15 times the minimum subsistence amount (existential minimum) for the first month and 2 times for each following month. It is recommended to confirm the current amount with the Czech representation where you will apply.

Working while studying

Non-EU students who study in an accredited full-time program can work without a work permit; the employer must report the work to the Labour Office.

Work permit after graduation

Non-EU graduates can apply for a residence permit valid for up to 9 months for job seeking or starting a business.

Academic calendar

The academic year has two semesters: the winter semester starts in October and the summer semester in February. Each semester normally has 15 weeks of teaching, followed by an exam period.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How does a student who does not know Czech prepare for Czech-language programs that have no tuition fee?

ÚJOP, the institute of Charles University, runs three-semester preparatory programs. For programs starting in September 2027, the fee is CZK 122,330 for Humanities PROGRESS and CZK 172,420 for Medicine and Pharmacy COMPLEX. According to ÚJOP, Czech at B1 level is enough for many faculties.

Where is the visa application made, and how long does it take?

Long-term visa and residence permit applications are made only at the Embassy in Ankara or the Consulate General in Istanbul. In Ankara, the appointment is requested by email with the admission letter and a passport copy, and it is given in order of arrival. A study visa is issued for up to 12 months; the decision comes within 60 days.

How much money do we need to show in the bank?

15 times the minimum subsistence amount is shown for the first month and 2 times for each following month. This amount is currently CZK 3,130 a month; for a 12-month stay it comes to CZK 115,810 in total. ÚJOP of Charles University writes that this is about €5,000.

Is it possible to study medicine in English in Czechia?

Yes, there are English-taught medicine programs. At Palacký University, applications for the September 2027 intake open on 1 November 2026. The entrance exam is online and the application fee is €80.

How is our Turkish high school diploma recognised in Czechia?

There are two routes: nostrification at the regional office (krajský úřad), or recognition made at admission by a university with institutional accreditation. Recognition by the university is valid only for that university and faculty. The legal time for nostrification is 30 days, and in complex cases it can extend to 60 days.
