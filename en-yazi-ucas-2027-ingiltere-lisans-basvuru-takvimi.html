UCAS 2027: bachelor's application calendar in the United Kingdom

Applications

Undergraduate applications in the United Kingdom are made through UCAS. Below are the key dates UCAS has announced for 2027 entry.

HistoryWhat's on
12 January 2027Oxford undergraduate results are released through UCAS (candidates invited to interview, 2027 entry)
13 January 2027UCAS 2027 entry: equal consideration deadline
25 February 2027UCAS Extra opens
2 June 2027UCAS: deadline to reply to offers if all replies arrived by 12 May
30 June 2027UCAS final application deadline (after this, applications go to Clearing)
2 July 2027UCAS Clearing opens
23 September 2027UCAS final application deadline for 2027 entry

Times are UK time. The application deadline for Oxford, Cambridge and most medicine, dentistry and veterinary programs was 15 October 2026.
