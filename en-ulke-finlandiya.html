Finland

Most English-taught bachelor's and master's programs in Finland are entered through the joint application in January. In 2026 this period was 7 to 21 January, and you could apply to up to six programs with one form. Applicants from outside the EU have paid a 100 EUR application fee since 1 January 2025.

FINLAND

Studying in Finland

01

How does a Turkish high school diploma open the door?

At Aalto University, a student applying with a high school diploma from outside Finland is assessed by their SAT or ACT result. A student with an IB or EB diploma competes in a separate round using diploma grades. The diploma must also give the right to enter university in Türkiye. The University of Helsinki also has a separate group for SAT/ACT applicants. The 2027 application runs from 5 to 19 January.

02

One portal for applications: Studyinfo.fi

For English-taught bachelor's and master's programs starting in autumn 2027, the joint application is made on Studyinfo.fi between 7 and 21 January 2027. Applicants from outside the EU, the EEA and Switzerland pay a 100 euro application fee. In the joint application, only one study place in Finland can be accepted in a term.

03

Tuition fees and one study year

There are no tuition fees in Finnish and Swedish programs. In English-taught programs, non-EU students pay tuition. According to Study in Finland, the annual fee is usually between 9,000 and 20,000 euros. A bachelor's takes 3 years at a university and 3.5 to 4.5 years at a university of applied sciences (UAS). At the University of Helsinki, the first teaching period of 2027/28 starts on 6 September 2027.

04

Working while studying

With a study residence permit you can work for pay in any field. The limit is 30 hours a week on average. Some weeks can go over 30 hours, as long as the yearly average does not pass this limit. Internships and the thesis that are part of the program do not count toward this limit. Since 15 April 2022, there is also no right to unlimited work during holidays.

05

After graduation

A student who graduates in Finland can apply for a residence permit to look for work or start a company. This permit is given for up to two years. You can apply within five years after your study permit ends. For the two-year permit, you need to show 800 euros a month, 19,200 euros in total.

FINLAND

Important for Finland

01

Entrance exam at universities of applied sciences

Universities of applied sciences (UAS) can use an entrance exam in the joint application. For example, Savonia selects students with the International UAS Exam for its spring 2026 intake. Those who want to enter with an SAT score use Savonia's direct application route instead of the joint application. LAB also asks you to check each program's exam information separately.

02

The number of applications halved after the application fee

In the first period after the application fee was introduced, about 32,400 people applied in the spring 2025 joint application. In 2024 this number was about 63,000. In the same period, there were about 8,600 starting places in 431 programs.

03

No English-taught medicine; 9,600 EUR in the bank

In Finland, medicine is taught only in Finnish or Swedish. For study of one year or longer, you need 9,600 EUR in your bank account at the time of application. Students can work for pay in any field for up to 30 hours a week on average.

IN BRIEF

Finland in brief

Bachelor'sMaster'sGeneral

Last checked: October 2026. Rules and amounts can change. We check the current situation together before you apply.

Bachelor's: length and application route

A bachelor's takes 3 years at universities and 3.5 to 4.5 years at universities of applied sciences (UAS). Applications are made through the official portal Studyinfo.fi. The main joint application period for English-taught programs is January, for study that starts the following autumn. Medicine is not taught in English. The January 2026 joint application period ran from 7 to 21 January.

Master's: length and application route

A master's takes 2 years at universities. UAS master's programs take about 1 to 1.5 years and require two years of related work experience. Applications are made through Studyinfo.fi, and some programs have their own application periods.

Language of instruction

Besides Finnish and Swedish programs, there are about 600 bachelor's and master's programs taught in English. Some fields, such as medicine, are taught only in Finnish or Swedish.

Tuition fees

Students from outside the EU/EEA and Switzerland usually pay tuition for English-taught bachelor's and master's programs. Finnish or Swedish programs have no tuition fee. Fees are roughly between 9,000 and 20,000 EUR a year, and since 1 January 2025 these students also pay a 100 euro application fee.

Student visa

The document is called 'Residence permit for studies', and the application is made through the online Enter Finland system. After applying, you need to go to an appointment at the nearest Finnish embassy or consulate for identity verification.

Proof of funds

For study shorter than one year, you must show 800 EUR a month. For study of one year or longer, you need 9,600 EUR in your bank account at the time of application. If the tuition fee will be paid after the application, you are also asked to show the first year's fee separately.

Working while studying

Students can work for pay in any field for up to 30 hours a week on average. During holidays, full-time work is possible as long as the yearly average does not go over this limit.

Work permit after graduation

After graduation, you can apply for a residence permit to look for work or start a business. Study in Finland states that this permit is for two years.

Academic calendar

The main joint applications are for study that starts the following autumn. A limited number of English-taught programs start in January, and a separate joint application period opens in autumn for them.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I study in Finland without knowing Finnish?

Yes. According to Study in Finland, the country has about 600 English-taught bachelor's and master's programs. For example, Aalto University has six English-taught bachelor's programs. Finnish and Swedish programs have no tuition fee, but they require you to know that language.

How much money do we need to show in the bank for the residence permit?

For study of one year or longer, the student must have 9,600 euros in their account at the time of application. If the tuition fee will be paid later, the first year's fee must also be in the account separately, because living money does not count toward tuition. For example, for a bachelor's student admitted to Tampere in 2027, this means 10,000 euros on top of the 9,600 euros.

Where do I apply for the residence permit in Türkiye?

The application is filled in online through the Enter Finland system. Applicants from Türkiye book an appointment at the VFS Global residence permit center in Ankara for identity verification. The application fee is not refunded even if the application is rejected or cancelled.

What kind of health insurance do I need?

The insurance must be valid on the day you arrive in Finland, and its deductible can be at most 300 euros. For study shorter than two years, the insurance must cover at least 120,000 euros of treatment costs. For study of two years or longer, it must cover at least 40,000 euros of medicine costs.

Can we apply to more than one Finnish university in the same year?

In the joint application you can apply to more than one program, but only one study place in Finland can be accepted in a term. Aalto and the University of Helsinki use their own calendars. Helsinki's SAT/ACT application runs from 5 to 19 January 2027, and Aalto's from 7 to 22 January 2027. The application fee is 100 euros for non-EU applicants.
