History

History

The history department studies the economies, societies, beliefs and cultures that shaped the world over time. Evaluating sources, evidence-based analysis and building written arguments are at the center of the program.

Example courses

Societies and economies in world historyThe making of EuropeHistory and politics of the modern Middle EastTheory and practice of oral historyEvidence and method

What do graduates do?

  • Archivist
  • Museum and gallery curator
  • Cultural heritage manager
  • Academic and researcher
  • Teacher
  • Journalist
  • Policy specialist
  • Archaeologist
IN HIGH SCHOOL

Which subjects matter?

The University of Oxford asks for AAA at A-level for the history bachelor's and sets no compulsory subject. However, it strongly recommends taking history at A-level or IB Higher Level.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does Oxford ask for an exam or an assignment when you apply for history?

For 2027 entry, Oxford does not ask for a written exam in history. Instead it asks for an essay on a history topic, written at school, of up to 2,000 words; the deadline is 10 November 2026. Applicants who pass the first screening are invited to at least one online interview in December.

source
Can you study history in English in the Netherlands?

It depends on the university. In the University of Groningen's history bachelor's, all courses of the international program are in English and the program takes 3 years (180 EC). The history bachelor's at Leiden University is in Dutch.
