Canada

University programs in Canada are taught in English or French. Most study permit applications ask for an attestation letter from the province (PAL/TAL). A student going to Quebec also gets a CAQ first. A study permit is not needed for language courses of six months or less.

CANADA

Studying in Canada

01

High school: study permit and December applications

A minor student who will study for 6 months or longer without a parent must get a study permit before entering Canada. Ridley College has announced 1 December 2026 as the first review date for boarding applicants. Upper Canada College also accepts Year 9 applications until 1 December and announces its decisions on 26 February 2027.

02

University: each school has its own rules, and for most the deadline is 15 January

In Canada, each university sets its own admission requirements. Applications to universities in Ontario go through the OUAC portal, and the form used for the University of Toronto is OUAC 105. For Fall 2027, the application deadline at the University of Toronto, UBC and McGill is 15 January 2027. Toronto recommends applying by 7 November 2026 for the first review rounds.

03

Quota and provincial attestation letter

Canada limits the number of new international students. The target for 2026 is 155,000 people. This is why most study permit applications ask for a provincial attestation letter (PAL/TAL), which shows that a place has been set aside in the quota. Students accepted to a graduate degree program at public institutions are exempt from this letter. A student going to Quebec first gets a CAQ from the province.

04

Working while studying

While classes are in session, you can work up to 24 hours a week off campus. This limit went up from 20 to 24 hours on 15 November 2024. There is no hour limit during planned school breaks such as summer and winter holidays.

05

After graduation

Eligible graduates can get a Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP) once. This work permit lasts up to three years.

CANADA

What matters for Canada

01

A shared application portal in Ontario, British Columbia and Alberta

In Canada, each university sets its own admission requirements. Some provinces, such as Ontario, British Columbia and Alberta, have a shared online application portal. In Ontario, this portal is called the Ontario Universities' Application Centre (OUAC).

IN BRIEF

Canada in brief

High schoolLanguage and summer schoolBachelor'sGeneral

Last checked: October 2026. Rules and amounts can change. We check the current situation together before you apply.

High school: international students

Many public high schools, as well as private and boarding schools, accept international students. Students who do not stay with their family can stay with a host family (homestay) or at a boarding school. Minor students at high school level (K-12) do not have to submit a provincial attestation letter (PAL/TAL) with their study permit application.

Language schools and summer schools

Accredited language programs are members of the national association Languages Canada. Language schools also offer seasonal events and camp programs. A study permit is not needed for language courses of six months or less.

Bachelor's: length and application route

Each university sets its own admission requirements, and applications are made by program. Some provinces, such as Ontario, British Columbia and Alberta, have central application portals. In Ontario, this portal is the Ontario Universities' Application Centre.

Language of instruction

University programs in Canada are taught in English or French, and some universities teach in both languages.

Tuition fees

University fees in Canada change by program and school. The official source does not give a general fee range for international students.

Student visa

To study in Canada, you need a study permit. Most applications ask for a provincial or territorial attestation letter (PAL/TAL). Graduate degree students at public institutions are exempt from this requirement.

Proof of funds

For applications made on or after 1 September 2026, a single applicant who will study outside Quebec must show at least 23,448 CAD for living costs. First-year tuition and travel costs are not included in this amount. They are shown separately.

Working while studying

During the term, you can work up to 24 hours a week off campus. There is no hour limit during planned school breaks such as summer and winter holidays.

Work permit after graduation

Eligible graduates can get a Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP) once, for up to three years.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

In which language are studies in Canada taught, and is the process different in Quebec?

University programs are taught in English or French, and some universities teach in both languages. A student admitted to a school in Quebec first applies for a CAQ from the province's immigration ministry, MIFI, and then applies for the federal study permit. Programs of six months or less are exempt from the CAQ requirement.

Is a custodian required in Canada for high school?

A student under 17 either comes with a parent or names a custodian in Canada. The custodian must be an adult who is a Canadian citizen or permanent resident. The custodian gets the Custodianship Declaration (IMM 5646) form notarized in Canada, and the parents get it notarized in Türkiye.

Are there other payments besides boarding school fees?

Brentwood College School asks new students for a 5,000 CAD registration fee and a 25,000 CAD deposit on acceptance. At Upper Canada College, the one-time registration fee for a boarding student is 7,500 CAD. If the study permit is refused and the school is told before 1 August, Brentwood refunds the tuition and registration fee in full.

Does university tuition go up every year?

It depends on the school. McGill says the tuition of an international bachelor's student admitted in 2026/27 stays fixed for the whole program. The condition is not to take a break and not to change programs. At UBC, the Master of Data Science installment is 19,156.39 CAD for 2025 entry and 19,922.64 CAD for 2026 entry.

Ask your question on WhatsApp
Do you need a visa for summer school or a language course?

A study permit is not needed for language courses of six months or less. But Türkiye is on Canada's list of countries that need a visa, so a student with Turkish citizenship travels with a visitor visa (TRV). Schools such as ILSC leave the visa process to the family, but they include the custodian letter for minor students in the package.
