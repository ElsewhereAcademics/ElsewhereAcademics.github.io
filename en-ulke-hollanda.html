Netherlands

A bachelor's at a research university in the Netherlands takes 3 years, and at a university of applied sciences (hogeschool) it takes 4 years. Applications are made through the government's joint platform Studielink. For most bachelor's programs, the deadline is 1 May. For numerus fixus programs with limited places, the deadline is 15 January and there is a selection.

NETHERLANDS

Studying in the Netherlands

01

Which door does a Turkish high school diploma open?

Nuffic considers a Turkish high school diploma equal to at least HAVO level in the Netherlands. Research universities add further requirements. For 2027/28 entry, Leiden asks for a diploma grade of at least 80 from a general high school (Genel Lise). In the social sciences, the math grade must be above 80 every year. If it is not, an online math exam is required.

02

University or hogeschool?

A bachelor's at a research university takes three years, at 180 ECTS. At a hogeschool, teaching focuses on putting knowledge into practice, and a bachelor's takes four years. A master's at both types of institution is at least one year, 60 ECTS.

03

One door for applications: Studielink

Bachelor's applications are made through the government's joint system, Studielink. For numerus fixus programs with limited places, such as medicine, the deadline for September 2027 entry is 15 January 2027. The general deadline is 1 May, but at UvA the deadline for non-EU students with foreign diplomas is 1 April 2027.

04

Work while studying and monthly living money

Students from outside the EU can work up to 16 hours a week during term, or only in the summer months. For the residence permit, in 2026 the IND asks university and hogeschool students for living money of at least 1,130.77 euros a month. Universities can ask for a higher amount in their own calculations. Groningen states 1,180 euros a month for 2026.

05

A one-year job search permit after graduation

Graduates can get a zoekjaar permit of up to one year to look for work. You apply for this permit within 3 years after graduation, and the permit cannot be extended.

NETHERLANDS

Important for the Netherlands

01

Numerus fixus: limited places, 15 January

Some programs, such as medicine, have limited places. These are called numerus fixus programs. The Studielink deadline for these programs is 15 January, and you can apply to at most two of them in one year. After this date, universities run their own selection. In 2026, results were announced through Studielink after 15 April.

IN BRIEF

The Netherlands in brief

Bachelor'sMaster'sGeneral

Last checked: October 2026. Rules and amounts can change. We check the current situation together before you apply.

Bachelor's: length and application route

A bachelor's takes 3 years (180 ECTS) at research universities and 4 years (240 ECTS) at universities of applied sciences. Application and enrolment are made through Studielink, the joint platform for state higher education institutions. For most bachelor's programs the deadline is 1 May, and for numerus fixus programs it is 15 January.

Master's: length and application route

Master's programs take at least 1 year (60 ECTS) at both research universities and universities of applied sciences, and can go up to 2 years depending on the program. Studielink, the joint platform of state institutions, is used for enrolment.

Language of instruction

A large part of higher education programs are taught in Dutch. Besides these, more than 1,800 programs are offered fully in English.

Tuition fees

The tuition fee paid by non-EU students varies from institution to institution. For example, Inholland states an annual fee of 10,360 euros for international students in bachelor's programs starting in September 2026.

Student visa

The residence permit for study in the Netherlands is called a temporary residence permit for study purposes (VVR) and is issued by the IND. Inholland states the IND application fee as 243 euros.

Proof of funds

Each year, the student must show enough financial means for living costs, tuition and health insurance. For a September 2026 start, Inholland states living costs as 1,100 euros a month, and at least 13,200 euros for the first year.

Working while studying

Non-EU students can work up to 16 hours a week during term with a special work permit obtained by the employer. Full-time work is possible during the summer holiday.

Work permit after graduation

Graduates can get a 'residence permit for orientation year' (zoekjaar hoogopgeleiden) of up to 1 year to look for work. You can apply within 3 years after finishing your studies, and the permit cannot be extended.

Academic calendar

The academic year starts on 1 September. Institutions usually work with two semesters, and each institution sets its own term structure.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can a graduate of an İmam Hatip, vocational or open high school apply?

For 2027/28, Leiden evaluates İmam Hatip, vocational and technical high school diplomas only together with university results. An Open Education High School diploma is reviewed case by case. In this case, you may be advised to start at a hogeschool first.

Who makes the visa and residence permit application?

In the Netherlands, the university makes the MVV and residence permit application on behalf of the student. The student sends the documents and the fee to the school. After approval, the MVV is collected by appointment from the Dutch consulate chosen in the application. According to VU, the passport is handed in and the visa is placed in the passport within 10 working days.

How much money do we need to show in the bank account for the visa?

For 2026, the IND asks university and hogeschool students for at least 1,130.77 euros a month. Schools can set their own amount. Groningen gives 14,160 euros for 12 months for 2026. This money is calculated separately from tuition.

How many numerus fixus programs can we apply to?

You can enrol in at most two numerus fixus programs in one academic year. Universities run their own selection after 15 January. For September 2027 entry, ranking numbers are announced in Studielink after 15 April 2027.

How different is the master's tuition fee from the bachelor's?

The difference is big, even at the same school. At Erasmus Rotterdam for 2027/28, an economics bachelor's costs a non-EU student 14,700 euros a year and a master's 22,800 euros. At UvA, the amounts for economics and business in the same year are 15,730 and 24,500 euros.
