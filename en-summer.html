When should you start?

A month-by-month plan for 2027 entry, by country. Official date Our suggestion

  1. August 2026

    St Giles published the dates and prices of its 2027 junior summer programs (brochure dated 28 August 2026).

  2. November 2026 to January 2027

    Choose the school and center by age, English level and city.

  3. February to March 2027

    Register and pay the course deposit. For St Giles junior programs, the deposit is £100 per week.

  4. March to April 2027

    Apply for the Standard Visitor visa. Türkiye is on the list of countries that need a visa in advance to travel to the United Kingdom for any purpose.

  5. April to May 2027

    Prepare flights, the airport transfer and the parent consent documents the school asks for.

  6. 20 June to 4 July 2027

    St Giles junior programs start: Brighton 20 June, London 27 June, Cambridge, Oxford and Canterbury 4 July.

  7. 1 to 8 August 2027

    Programs end: London and Brighton 1 August, Cambridge, Oxford and Canterbury 8 August.

    Complete the registration and ask the school for registration and payment documents for your visa file.

  4. January 2027

    You can apply for the visa at the earliest 6 months before travel. If you will travel in early July, applications open in early January.

  5. April 2027

    Book a VFS Global appointment. For ages 12 and over, the visa fee is 90 euros and the service fee is 30 euros. Payment is made in cash euros.

  6. May 2027

    Because processing can take up to 45 days, for a trip in early July we recommend submitting your application by mid-May. Applications are not accepted less than 15 days before travel.

  7. June 2027

    Plan your flight according to the 2027 start date the school will announce. The C visa covers 7 days in addition to the length of the course.

ELSEWHERESUMMER FAQ

Frequently asked questions

All questions

From what age can you join summer school?

Maltalingua's 2026 junior packages are split into age groups 8 to 12 and 13 to 17. Embassy Summer's 2026 homestay program is for students aged 13 to 17. For adult courses, EC Dubai and English Path Dubai set the minimum age at 16.

Ask your question on WhatsApp
What is usually included in the price of a junior summer program?

Maltalingua's 2026 packages include an English course, full board, activities, 24/7 supervision, return airport transfers and insurance. In Embassy Summer's homestay program, lessons, transport, activities, accommodation and full board are included in the price. In the 2027 summer course of SSHL in Sweden too, lessons, room, meals and activities are included in the fee of SEK 39,900.

Ask your question on WhatsApp
How many hours of lessons are there per week in a summer course?

At BELS Malta, IH Malta and Embassy Summer, the standard course is 20 lessons a week. At Embassy Summer a lesson lasts 45 minutes. At IH Malta and BELS, 20 lessons add up to 15 hours in total. At Embassy, lessons are held in the morning or the afternoon, and there are morning, afternoon and evening activities every day.

Ask your question on WhatsApp
Can you earn university credit (ECTS) in summer school?

Some universities' summer schools offer courses with ECTS credit. Aarhus University's 2027 AU Summer University courses are one example. At Humboldt University's summer school, you must attend at least 80% of classes to earn credit. Whether the credit counts at your own university is decided by that university.

ElsewhereCampus Ask your question on WhatsApp
Where does the student stay during a summer course?

Maltalingua's 2026 junior packages have homestay, standard residence and superior residence options. In Embassy Summer's homestay program, the student stays in a room for two, three or four people, and breakfast and dinner are eaten with the host family. Maltalingua charges a supervision fee of €80 a week for students aged 8 to 12.

JOURNEY

How do we work?

Five steps from the first meeting to the end of summer.

  1. 1

    First meeting

    We talk about the student's age, English level, interests and summer plans.

  2. 2

    Choosing a programme

    We share programmes that suit the age, length and budget, with why we recommend each one.

  3. 3

    Enrolment and documents

    We complete the enrolment form and payment schedule with the school; if a visa is needed, we prepare the documents.

  4. 4

    Getting ready to travel

    We go through flights, airport transfer, insurance and the parental consent forms the school asks for.

  5. 5

    During the summer

    While the student is there, we stay in touch with the school and the family.
