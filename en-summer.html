ELSEWHERESUMMER

This summer, somewhere else.

01

For most students, summer school is the first meeting with life abroad.

02

In the mornings you learn the language in class. In the afternoons you explore a new city and meet friends from all over the world.

03

We choose the program together, based on the student's age, language level and interests.

Who is it for?

For students who want to spend a summer abroad, at a language school or in a university summer program.

ELSEWHERESUMMER FAQ

Frequently asked questions

All questions

From what age can you join summer school?

Maltalingua's 2026 junior packages are split into age groups 8 to 12 and 13 to 17. Embassy Summer's 2026 homestay program is for students aged 13 to 17. For adult courses, EC Dubai and English Path Dubai set the minimum age at 16.

source · source
What is usually included in the price of a junior summer program?

Maltalingua's 2026 packages include an English course, full board, activities, 24/7 supervision, return airport transfers and insurance. In Embassy Summer's homestay program, lessons, transport, activities, accommodation and full board are included in the price. In the 2027 summer course of SSHL in Sweden too, lessons, room, meals and activities are included in the fee of SEK 39,900.

source · source
How many hours of lessons are there per week in a summer course?

At BELS Malta, IH Malta and Embassy Summer, the standard course is 20 lessons a week. At Embassy Summer a lesson lasts 45 minutes. At IH Malta and BELS, 20 lessons add up to 15 hours in total. At Embassy, lessons are held in the morning or the afternoon, and there are morning, afternoon and evening activities every day.

source · source
Can you earn university credit (ECTS) in summer school?

Some universities' summer schools offer courses with ECTS credit. Aarhus University's 2027 AU Summer University courses are one example. At Humboldt University's summer school, you must attend at least 80% of classes to earn credit. Whether the credit counts at your own university is decided by that university.

source · source
Where does the student stay during a summer course?

Maltalingua's 2026 junior packages have homestay, standard residence and superior residence options. In Embassy Summer's homestay program, the student stays in a room for two, three or four people, and breakfast and dinner are eaten with the host family. Maltalingua charges a supervision fee of €80 a week for students aged 8 to 12.

source · source
JOURNEY

How do we work?

Eight steps, from the first meeting to the first week after the student arrives.

  1. 1

    First meeting

    We listen first. We do not talk about schools before we understand the student's interests, goals and the family's expectations.

  2. 2

    Roadmap

    After setting the goal, we draw the path to it. We share country, field of study and school options with you, together with why we suggest each one.

  3. 3

    Budget plan

    We put the paths that lead to the same goal with different budgets side by side. We make clear from the start which payment is due when.

  4. 4

    Exams and documents

    We build the calendar of exams the schools ask for and prepare the documents step by step.

  5. 5

    Application

    We prepare the file each school asks for in full and complete the applications according to each school's own calendar. We regularly share with you which document was sent to which school and when.

  6. 6

    Decision

    When acceptances arrive, we put the options side by side and make the final decision together, keeping the student's goal in mind.

  7. 7

    Orientation

    Orientation starts with the acceptance. We prepare the student for the new city, the school culture and the first weeks. Depending on your package, visa, accommodation and travel planning are also part of this stage.

  8. 8

    After arrival

    The first mornings in a new city feel a little strange. At the end of the first week, we have a meeting with our student: classes, housing, new friends, suggestions.
