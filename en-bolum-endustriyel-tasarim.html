Industrial Design / Product Design

Industrial Design / Product Design

Industrial design teaches product design by combining creativity with function. Materials, visualization techniques, ergonomics and engineering analysis for product design are among the main subjects.

Example courses

MaterialsVisualization techniquesContextual studies and ergonomicsEngineering analysis for product designBasic electronics for product design

What do graduates do?

  • Product designer
  • Industrial designer
  • UX designer
  • Furniture designer
  • CAD technician
  • Exhibition designer
  • Innovation consultant
  • Product manager
IN HIGH SCHOOL

Which subjects matter?

TU Delft asks for mathematics and physics in high school for its bachelor's programs, including Industrial Design Engineering. This program also sets a Dutch proficiency requirement before the selection process.

Example: University of the Arts London (UAL), first-year courses

  1. Unit 1: Introduction to Study Product and Industrial Design in Higher Education
  2. Unit 2: Establishing Individual Design Skills (Platform-Aligned)
  3. Unit 3: Developing Design Skills (across Platforms)
  4. Unit 4: Creative Unions

Length by country

United Kingdom
UAL CSM BA Product and Industrial Design 3 years; 4 years with the Diploma in Professional Studies.
United Kingdom
Cambridge Design: BA 3 years or MDes 4 years.
USA
A bachelor's degree is usually completed in 4 years of full-time study.
Netherlands
A bachelor's at a research university takes 3 years (180 ECTS). At an HBO (university of applied sciences) it takes 4 years (240 ECTS).

Exams required for this field

Portfolio United Kingdom

UAL Central Saint Martins BA Product and Industrial Design asks for a digital portfolio of up to 25 pages. It should also include photos of three-dimensional work, which does not have to be related to product design.

College assessment and portfolio United Kingdom

In the Cambridge Design program, all colleges hold an assessment. Applicants invited to an interview bring their portfolio.

TOLD (Test del Design) Italy

TOLD is used for entry to Politecnico di Milano design bachelor's degrees for 2026/27.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can you study industrial design in English in Europe?

There are examples. Eindhoven University of Technology's Industrial Design bachelor's is in English and takes 3 years. In Hungary, Óbuda University's Industrial Design Engineering program is in English, with 7 semesters and 210 ECTS. TU Delft's program asks for Dutch proficiency.

How important is a portfolio when applying for industrial design?

At Óbuda University, the portfolio makes up at most half of the admission score, and there are also written exams that include mathematics. UAL Central Saint Martins asks for a digital portfolio of up to 25 pages and also expects photos of three-dimensional work.

Does the program include a year in industry?

UAL Central Saint Martins' Product and Industrial Design bachelor's takes 3 years. If the Diploma in Professional Studies year is added, the program becomes 4 years.
