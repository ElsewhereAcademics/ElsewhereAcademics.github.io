English Level Assessment

A short online test. We will send you your estimated English level within 24 hours.

40questions
5levels: A1, A2, B1, B2, C1
60seconds: time for each question
3sections: grammar, vocabulary, reading

Before you start

  • The test measures your grammar, vocabulary and reading skills. Listening and speaking are not part of this test.
  • The questions go from easy to hard. You have 60 seconds for each question. When the time is up, the test moves on to the next question.
  • You cannot go back and change an answer. If you do not know an answer, you can leave it blank with the “I don’t know, skip” button. Leaving it blank gives a more accurate estimate than guessing.
  • We suggest taking it in a quiet place, in one sitting. The test takes about 25 to 40 minutes.
  • When the test ends, you enter your name, phone and email. We will send you your estimated level by phone or email within 24 hours. You can find out how your information is processed in the KVKK privacy notice.
  • The result is only an estimate and does not replace an official exam result.
