ITALY

Studying in Italy

01

The application goes through Universitaly

A student who needs a visa pre-registers on the Universitaly portal for the program they apply to. Once the university confirms the pre-registration, a visa appointment is booked. In 2026/27 and 2027/28, a B2 language level is asked for Italian or English programs, and B1 is enough for Foundation programs.

02

The entrance exam varies by university

Politecnico di Milano accepted a TOL, TOLC-I, CEnT-S or SAT result for 2026/27 engineering bachelor's entry, and for design programs it uses TOLD. Bocconi asks for an online test for bachelor's applications, and Luiss runs a separate test for non-EU applicants. For English-language medicine there is the IMAT: a 60-question paper exam. The 2026 exam was held on 29 September.

03

How a study year goes

A bachelor's (laurea) is three years and 180 ECTS, and a master's (laurea magistrale) is two years and 120 ECTS. The academic year mostly starts in October, and each university sets its own start date. Some programs are a single five-year cycle. Building Engineering/Architecture at Politecnico di Milano is one of them and is taught in Italian.

04

Daily life as a student

With a study residence permit, you can work up to 20 hours a week and up to 1,040 hours a year. In Milan, the monthly public transport pass for students under 27 is €22. In Bologna, a single room in a shared flat averages €400 a month plus bills, according to the university.

ITALY

Key points for Italy

01

Universitaly first, then the visa

Candidates who need a visa first pre-register on the Universitaly portal for the program they apply to. Once the university confirms the pre-registration, a visa appointment is booked. For the university enrollment visa, at least €10,179.85 a year in funds is shown for 2026/2027 and 2027/2028.

02

IMAT for English-language medicine

The IMAT exam is used to enter English-language medicine programs. The exam is in English and on paper. It has 60 multiple-choice questions and the top score is 90. Registration is through Universitaly, and in 2026 registration was open from 26 August to 9 September. Applicants living outside the EU compete in a separate ranking.

03

B2 language requirement from 2026

In 2026/2027 and 2027/2028, you apply to Italian or English programs with a B2 language certificate. B1 is enough for Foundation programs.

IN BRIEF

Italy in brief

Bachelor'sMaster'sGeneral

Last checked: October 2026. Rules and amounts can change. We check the current situation together before you apply.

Bachelor's: length and application route

A bachelor's degree (laurea) takes three years and is worth 180 ECTS credits. Applicants who need a student visa first pre-register on the Universitaly portal. For 2026/2027, the deadline for bachelor's and master's visa applications is 30 November 2026.

Master's: length and application route

A master's degree (laurea magistrale) takes two years and is worth 120 ECTS credits. The application route is the same as for a bachelor's: you pre-register on Universitaly and apply for the visa after the institution approves it.

Language of instruction

Programs can be taught in Italian or English. For 2026/2027 and 2027/2028, the required language level is B2 for programs in Italian or English and B1 for Foundation programs.

Student visa

To enroll at a university, you need a student visa (visto per studio / immatricolazione). You can book a visa appointment after the institution approves your Universitaly pre-registration.

Proof of funds

For university enrollment visa applications in the 2026/2027 and 2027/2028 academic years, you must show financial means of at least €10,179.85 per year. This amount does not include a return ticket or unpaid fees.

Working while studying

A study residence permit gives you the right to work part-time for up to 20 hours a week and up to 1,040 hours a year.

Academic calendar

The academic year usually starts in October. The start date and term structure are decided by each institution.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much money should show in your bank account for the visa?

In 2026/27 and 2027/28, at least €10,179.85 per year must be shown for a university enrollment visa. A return ticket and fees that have not been paid yet are not counted in this amount.

Why do public university fees differ from one family to another?

Public universities such as Politecnico di Milano and Bologna calculate fees based on family income. If the family has income abroad, a separate income calculation is requested. If you do not submit the documents, Bologna charges the highest amount for the program.

Can a 16-year-old student in the final year of high school apply to a Bocconi bachelor's program?

For 2027/28 bachelor's entry, Bocconi says it cannot accept non-EU applicants who do not live in Italy if they were born after 1 September 2010. If your date of birth is close to this limit, it is a good idea to check this before you apply.

Which visa do you need for summer school or a short language course?

For courses of up to 90 days, you get a short-stay Schengen visa (type C). The application asks for proof of enrollment or pre-enrollment in the course in Italy.

Which official costs come up besides tuition and rent?

The University of Bologna says that for non-EU students, annual registration with the Italian health system (SSN) costs about €700, and renewing the residence permit costs about €120. The WAI ITALY health insurance listed on Bocconi's cost page is €140 for 1 year.
