Clinical Psychology

Clinical Psychology

Clinical psychology teaches you to assess and treat people with psychological difficulties linked to mental or physical health. It is studied after a bachelor's, at master's or doctoral level.

Health

What do graduates do?

  • Clinical psychologist
  • Hospitals and mental health services
  • Independent practice
  • Schools and universities
  • Research
GOOD TO KNOW

Before you apply

In the UK you first need a BPS accredited psychology degree, then a clinical psychology doctorate of about three years and HCPC registration. For the bachelor's, see our Psychology page.
