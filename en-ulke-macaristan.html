Hungary

In Hungary, you can study medicine, dentistry and pharmacy in English, and universities choose students with their own entrance exam. For the 6-year English-taught medicine program at Semmelweis University, there is an online test in biology, chemistry and English, followed by an interview. At the University of Debrecen, there is a written exam in chemistry or physics in addition to biology, and an English interview.

HUNGARY

Studying in Hungary

01

You apply directly to the university

A self-funded student sends their documents directly to the university they choose. The University of Debrecen asks for at least B2 in English from a supervised exam. In medicine, dentistry and pharmacy, the university chooses students with its own entrance exam and interview.

02

How does a study year work?

The academic year has two semesters, autumn and spring. At the ELTE Faculty of Humanities, 2026/27 autumn classes started on 7 September 2026, and exams end on 29 January 2027. Spring classes start on 8 February 2027, and the exam period closes on 2 July 2027. A bachelor's degree takes 6 to 8 semesters, which is 3 to 4 years.

03

Preparatory programs for health fields

Some universities open a preparatory program before health fields. For the University of Szeged's health sciences Foundation Year, the 2027/28 application deadline is 30 June 2027. Debrecen does not take students directly into medicine, dentistry and pharmacy for February 2027 entry.

04

Working while studying

With a residence permit for study, you can work up to 30 hours a week during the term. Outside the term, you have the right to work full-time for up to 90 days or 66 working days a year.

05

After graduation

A student who graduates in Hungary can get a residence permit valid for up to 9 months to look for a job or start a company. The application is made at least 15 days before the student permit ends. When a job is found, this permit can be changed into a residence permit for work.

What matters for Hungary

01

Semmelweis exam: the test from home, the interview in person or on Zoom

The Semmelweis written test is made up of medical biology, medical chemistry, general English and medical English, with 20 questions in each section. The student takes the test online from home on their own computer. The interview and oral exam are held in person or on Zoom. Exams are held once or twice a week from the start of March to the end of June.

02

Semmelweis fees are listed per semester

For English-taught medicine, Semmelweis states a fee of 9,340 EUR per semester, plus additional one-time fees. The page does not say which academic year this amount is for. The same page gives the last application date as 31 May 2027.

03

Biology is required at Debrecen, the second subject is your choice

At the University of Debrecen, the written exam for medicine, dentistry and pharmacy lasts 50 minutes. Biology is required for everyone. You choose chemistry or physics as the second subject. The interview assesses communication in English and motivation. The university has an information page made for Türkiye and a contact address in Istanbul.

IN BRIEF

Hungary at a glance

Bachelor'sMaster'sGeneral

Last checked: October 2026. Rules and amounts can change. We check the current situation together before you apply.

Bachelor's: length and application route

Bachelor's programs last 6 to 8 semesters (3 to 4 years). In fields like medicine there are single-stage programs of 10 to 12 semesters. Self-funded international students can send their documents directly to the institution. For programs that start in September, the second admission round is in February. For example, the English general medicine program at the University of Szeged lasts 12 semesters (360 ECTS).

Master's: length and application route

Master's programs last 2 to 4 semesters. Self-funded international students can send their application documents directly to the institution.

Language of instruction

Higher education institutions in Hungary offer more than 700 programs in English, German, French and other languages. Applications to Hungarian-language programs go through the FELVI system and require advanced Hungarian.

Tuition fees

Tuition fees vary by program and institution. For example, the University of Szeged lists a fee of 7,900 EUR per semester for its English general medicine program starting in autumn 2026/2027. Semmelweis University lists 9,340 euros per semester for English medicine, plus one-time fees.

Student visa

The document is called 'Residence Permit for the Purpose of Studies'. You apply at the Hungarian embassy or consulate in the country of your nationality. People who are already in Hungary without needing a visa can apply online through the Enter Hungary system.

Proof of funds

The applicant must show that they have enough financial means to cover living costs, accommodation, the return trip and health expenses. The official pages we reviewed do not state a fixed amount.

Working while studying

Students with a residence permit for study purposes can work up to 30 hours a week during the term. Outside the study periods, they can work full-time for up to 90 days a year (66 working days).

Work permit after graduation

While your study permit is still valid and the job you are looking for fits your level of study, you can apply for a residence permit for job seeking or starting a business.

Academic calendar

The academic year has two semesters, autumn and spring. Autumn semester registration takes place in the first half of September, and spring semester registration in the first half of February.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How does the entrance exam for medicine in Hungary work?

At Semmelweis there is a test with 20 questions each in medical biology, medical chemistry, general English and medical English. The student takes the test at home on their own computer. After that there is an interview, in person or on Zoom. At Debrecen, biology is required in the 50-minute written exam, and the second subject is chemistry or physics.

Is an English proficiency certificate required?

Debrecen asks for at least a B2 certificate from a supervised exam. Those who completed secondary school in English may be exempt with a language-of-instruction certificate. For the ELTE International Relations MA, B2 is required, and IELTS 5.5 or TOEFL iBT between 46 and 59 is accepted. At ELTE, a candidate without a certificate is assessed in an interview.

Is it possible to start in February?

At some universities, yes. At Debrecen, the last day for February 2027 entry is 1 November 2026. But in this intake there is no direct entry to medicine, dentistry or pharmacy. At BME, the application for February 2027 for self-funded students is between 15 October and 15 November 2026. The ELTE Faculty of Humanities calendar shows no bachelor's or master's entry for February 2027.

What is Stipendium Hungaricum, and when do you apply?

It is a scholarship from the Hungarian Government. It covers study with no tuition fee, a monthly stipend, an accommodation contribution and health insurance. The 2026/27 application opened on 14 November 2025 and closed on 15 January 2026 at 14:00 (CET). We could not yet find the 2027/28 dates on the official site.

How much money do we need to show in the bank for the residence permit?

The official pages we reviewed do not give a fixed amount. They ask for enough funds to cover your costs for the length of your stay. The required bank statement period varies by mission: the Hungarian Embassy in Kuwait asks for up to 6 months of statements, and the one in Pristina asks for up to 3 months. We recommend asking the mission in Türkiye for the current document list.
