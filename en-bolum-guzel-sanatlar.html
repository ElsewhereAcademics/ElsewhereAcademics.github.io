Fine Art

Fine Art

Fine art offers workshop-based education that develops creativity and personal expression. Besides studio practice, students also learn about art history, critical analysis and curating.

Example courses

Studio practiceArt historyReflection and analysisArt, technology and the internetCurating

  • Concept artist

  • Arts administrator
  • Graphic designer

IN HIGH SCHOOL

Which subjects matter?

The University of Edinburgh does not ask for a specific A-level or IB subject for fine art. Most applicants have ABB, or 34 points in the IB. Instead, a digital portfolio showing artistic skill is required.

Exams required for this field

Portfolio and interview United Kingdom

A portfolio is required for UCL Slade Fine Art: a PDF of up to 20 pages, with an optional video. Shortlisted applicants are invited to an interview at the end of February or in March.

Portfolio United Kingdom

UAL Central Saint Martins BA Fine Art assesses a digital portfolio of up to 25 pages for admission.

Artistic selection process Germany

At Berlin UdK, applicants prove their artistic ability, and admission is decided through a selection process. For pure art programs such as fine art, completing compulsory education (usually 10 years) is enough.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is a Foundation Diploma, and what is it for?

The Foundation Diploma in Art and Design is a one-year program that prepares you for an art and design bachelor's. According to Manchester Metropolitan University, its aim is to develop skills and a portfolio. UAL's program is also open to international students. UAL writes that those who pass the course are offered a place on one of its own bachelor's degrees.

Is a portfolio asked for when applying for fine art?

The Slade School of Fine Art, which is part of UCL, asks for one. All BA and BFA Fine Art applicants upload a single portfolio, and the invitation to upload comes after the UCAS application. Applicants who pass the first screening are invited to an interview. The BA takes 4 years and the BFA 3 years.
