FRANCE

Studying in France

01

Applications from Türkiye go through Campus France Türkiye

A student applying for the first year of a bachelor's prepares a preliminary admission file (DAP) through Campus France Türkiye and chooses up to three universities. For the 2027/28 term, the deadline for this file is 15 December 2026. Applications for the 2nd and 3rd years of a bachelor's and for master's programs follow the same route. The deadline is 30 December 2026. A foreign applicant living in Türkiye does not use the Mon Master platform in France.

source · source
02

French or English?

To enter a French-taught bachelor's program in the first year, a paid language exam is needed. For 2025 entry, Campus France Türkiye asked for at least B2 on the TCF. For a master's in a French-taught program, the school asks for DELF B2, DALF, TCF or TEF. For an English-taught program, IELTS, TOEFL or TOEIC is accepted. More than 1,600 programs in the Campus France catalog are partly or fully in English. Most of these are at master's level.

source · source
03

How a study year goes

A bachelor's (licence) takes three years. A master's is a 120-credit program of four semesters. The academic year starts in September or October, and the first semester ends in December or January. Each university sets its own calendar.

source · source
04

Daily life as a student

A student visa gives the right to work up to 964 hours a year. This is 60% of normal working time in France. A meal at a university restaurant costs 3.30 euros. In Paris, the Imagine'R student transport pass is 374.40 euros a year.

source · source
05

After graduation

A student who finishes a master's or a licence professionnelle can apply for a 12-month residence permit to look for work or start a company. This permit cannot be renewed. When you find a job, you need to switch to another residence permit before the time runs out.

source · source

FRANCE

Important for France

01

Preliminary admission file (DAP) for the first year

A preliminary admission application (DAP) is required for those applying to the first year with a high school diploma from a non-EU country. For the 2027/2028 term, the deadline for files is 15 December 2026. Up to three universities that you choose review the file at the same time. The deadline for the student to give their decision is 31 May 2027.

02

French level and exam exemption

Applicants whose native language is not French take a paid language exam, with some exceptions. Campus France Türkiye asks for the TCF for this exam, and its 2025 entry document looked for at least level B2. Graduates of schools on the list, such as Galatasaray High School, and holders of DELF B2 or DALF were exempt from the exam.

03

A separate tuition rate for non-EU students

At public universities, non-EU students pay 2,902 euros a year for a bachelor's and 3,950 euros for a master's in 2026/2027. A decree dated 19 May 2026 limited the share of students that universities can exempt from this fee.

04

964 hours a year while studying

In France, a student visa gives the right to work up to 964 hours a year. This equals 60% of normal annual working time in France.

IN BRIEF

France in brief

Bachelor'sMaster'sGeneral

Last checked: October 2026. Rules and amounts can change. We check the current situation together before you apply.

Bachelor's: length and application route

A bachelor's (licence) takes three years. Applicants living in Türkiye apply for the first year through Campus France Türkiye.

Master's: length and application route

A master's is a 120-credit program of four semesters. Together with the bachelor's, it adds up to 300 ECTS.

Tuition fees

For non-EU students at public universities, the 2026/2027 tuition fee is 2,902 euros a year for a bachelor's and 3,950 euros a year for a master's. A decree dated 19 May 2026 (n° 2026-385) limited the share of students that universities can exempt.

Student visa

The visa for study is a long-stay student visa that replaces a residence permit: 'visa long séjour valant titre de séjour mention étudiant' (VLS-TS étudiant). The visa allows a stay of four months to one year. It must be validated within three months after you arrive in France.

Proof of funds

The student must show resources of at least 615 euros a month.

Working while studying

A student visa gives the right to work up to 964 hours a year, which equals 60% of normal working time in France.

Work permit after graduation

Those who graduate with certain diplomas (licence professionnelle, master's, or a diploma at master's level or higher) can get a one-year residence permit to look for work or start a company. This permit cannot be renewed.

Academic calendar

The academic year starts in September or October. The first teaching period runs from September to December or January, and the second from January to April or June.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can a student who does not know French study in France?

Yes, there are English-taught programs. More than 1,600 programs in the Campus France catalog are partly or fully in English. When applying to these programs, you submit the IELTS, TOEFL or TOEIC certificate that the school asks for. There are fewer options in the first year of a bachelor's and more at master's level.

source · source
Can a student get housing aid (APL) in France?

Since 1 July 2026, a student from outside the EU who has no scholarship based on social criteria cannot get APL. A student who works or studies on an apprenticeship or professionalization contract can still apply. So it is more accurate to plan the family budget without housing aid.

source · source
How different is the tuition fee at a public university and at a school like Sciences Po?

In 2026/27 at a public university, a non-EU student pays 2,902 euros a year for a bachelor's and 3,950 euros for a master's. At Sciences Po in the same year, a family outside the EEA pays 14,900 euros for a bachelor's and 20,640 euros for a master's. Some public universities can exempt part of their non-EU students from this higher rate. You need to ask each university about this separately.

source · source
How many universities or programs can I apply to?

For the first year of a bachelor's, you choose up to three universities in the DAP file, and all three review the file at the same time. For the 2nd and 3rd years of a bachelor's and for master's programs, Campus France Türkiye allowed up to 7 programs for 2026 entry. Some schools, such as the grandes écoles, take applications on their own site.

source · source
Where do I apply for the visa in Türkiye?

After you confirm your final choice in the Campus France process, you download the pre-enrolment document (attestation de pré-inscription). With this document, the visa application is made directly to VFS Global. The visa you receive is called VLS-TS and replaces a residence permit.

source · source
