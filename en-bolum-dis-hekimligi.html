Dentistry

Dentistry

Dentistry studies the scientific basis of diseases of the mouth and teeth, and how to prevent and treat them. The program combines medical sciences, clinical skills and communication with patients. An important part of the training is hands-on clinical work.

Example courses

Oral biologyPeriodontologyPediatric dentistryPatient assessmentHealth ethics and law

What do graduates do?

  • Dentist
  • Oral health specialist (dental hygienist)
  • Dental therapist
  • Dental technician
  • Academic and researcher
  • Healthcare manager
  • Medical sales representative
IN HIGH SCHOOL

Which subjects matter?

For dentistry (BDS), King's College London asks for an A in biology or chemistry at A-level. The second subject must be one of biology, chemistry, physics, mathematics or psychology. For 2027 entry, the offer is A*AA.

Exams required for this field

UCAT United Kingdom

King's College London Dentistry BDS applicants sit the UCAT in the year they apply. The exam is held between July and September. For 2027 entry, the application deadline is 15 October 2026.

MedAT-Z Austria

Entry to dentistry at the medical universities of Vienna, Innsbruck and Graz is through the MedAT-Z. The 2026 exam was on 3 July.

Toelatingsexamen tandarts Belgium (Flemish region)

To start dentistry at Flemish universities, you have to pass the entrance exam and rank well. The 2027 exam is on 3 July, and registration runs from 1 March to 17 May 2027.

IMAT Italy

The entrance exam for English-taught medicine and dentistry programs at Italian state universities. For 2026/27, the exam date is 29 September 2026. 60 questions, 100 minutes.

Semmelweis entrance exam Hungary

At Semmelweis, dentistry applicants take tests in biology, chemistry, general English and medical English, and are then invited to an interview. Each test has 20 questions and lasts 20 minutes.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does a student studying dentistry abroad face the YKS requirement for equivalence (denklik)?

For students who finished high school in Türkiye, this depends on the university's ranking. If the university is not in the top 1,000 of at least two of the THE, QS, ARWU and CWTS Leiden lists, a YKS requirement applies. In that case, you need to have reached the band required for dentistry in the YKS of the year you started your studies.

source · source
Does a dentistry graduate take an exam for equivalence (denklik)?

If YÖK sees gaps or doubts in the core learning outcomes of the degree, it can decide on a level assessment exam (STS). ÖSYM holds the dentistry STS twice a year, and the passing score is 40 out of 100. A candidate who has not been given an exam decision can also take this exam if they wish.

source
Graduates of which universities get direct equivalence?

Direct equivalence (denklik) processing is done for institutions that are in the top 400 of at least three of the rankings accepted by YÖK, in the year the studies started. The accepted rankings are Times Higher Education, QS, ARWU and CWTS Leiden.

source
