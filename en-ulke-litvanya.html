Lithuania

In Lithuania, you can study medicine in English, and universities choose applicants with their own online exam. Vilnius University's English-taught medicine program takes 6 years (360 ECTS). At LSMU, the exam is made up of an interview and a multiple-choice test in biology and chemistry.

LITHUANIA

Studying in Lithuania

01

Applying with a Turkish high school diploma

Vilnius University asks a bachelor's applicant from Türkiye for a high school diploma and a transcript of the last four years. On top of these, a YKS result or proof of at least one year of university study is needed. You apply not through a central system but directly to the university that offers the program.

02

Program length and term start

Bachelor's programs are between 180 and 240 ECTS credits, and one study year counts as 60 ECTS. A master's degree needs 90 to 120 ECTS. Vytautas Magnus University gives 23 August 2027 as the preliminary start date for 2027/2028.

03

Fees differ by school and field

At Vilnius University, a non-EU student pays €4,350 a year for the Business and Finance bachelor's in 2026/2027. At Vilnius Tech, engineering and informatics bachelor's programs are €4,950 a year in the same year. At Vytautas Magnus University, the Business Administration bachelor's is €3,400 a year for 2027/2028.

04

Working while studying

A bachelor's student from outside the EU can work up to 20 hours a week in the first and second year. From the third year, the limit for bachelor's and master's students is 40 hours a week. These rules are in the law change that came into force on 22 May 2026.

05

After graduation

A student who graduates in Lithuania can get an additional residence permit to look for a job. The last student residence permit is usually given for six months longer than the study period.

LITHUANIA

What matters for Lithuania

01

LSMU exam: 30 biology and 30 chemistry questions

The LSMU entrance exam is held online: first an interview, then a 90-minute written test a few days later. The test has 30 questions each in biology and chemistry. Applicants with certain diplomas, such as A Level or IB, who meet the minimum grade in biology and chemistry may be exempt from the exam. For 2027 entry, applications open on 1 November 2026 and close on 6 July 2027.

02

English-taught medicine in Vilnius

At Vilnius University, medicine is taught in English and takes 6 years (360 ECTS credits). Applicants who must sit the exam take a compulsory online entrance exam. Biology at school is required.

IN BRIEF

Lithuania in brief

Bachelor'sMaster'sGeneral

Last checked: October 2026. Rules and amounts can change. We check the current situation together before you apply.

Bachelor's: length and application route

Bachelor's (first cycle) programs are 180 to 240 ECTS, and each study year corresponds to 60 ECTS. You apply directly to the university that offers the program. At LSMU, medicine is an integrated 6-year program. Vilnius University's English-taught medicine program takes 6 years (360 ECTS), and LSMU puts medicine applicants through an entrance test.

Master's: length and application route

A master's degree is given only by universities and needs 90 to 120 ECTS. You apply directly to the university.

Language of instruction

Programs listed on the Study in Lithuania portal are taught in English.

Tuition fees

Fees change by institution and program. Study in Lithuania states fees starting from 1,300 EUR a year for a bachelor's and from 2,300 EUR a year for a master's. At LSMU, the medicine fee for the 2026/2027 academic year is 12,800 EUR in years 1 to 3 and 13,300 EUR in years 4 to 6.

Student visa

Before coming to Lithuania, you need to apply for both a National visa D and a Temporary Residence Permit. Applications are made through the visa center (VFS Global) or the Lithuanian embassy.

Working while studying

Bachelor's students from outside the EU can work up to 20 hours a week in their first and second years and up to 40 hours a week in their third and fourth years. For master's and doctoral students, the limit is 40 hours a week.

Work permit after graduation

Study in Lithuania states that international students can stay in the country for 15 months after graduation to look for a job.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you go to Lithuania for a bachelor's degree with a visa or a residence permit?

Full-time bachelor's and master's students are not given a National visa D. They get a Temporary Residence Permit (TRP) directly. The application starts in MIGRIS, then you book an appointment at the VFS Global center in Türkiye. Normal processing takes up to 2 months.

Is it possible to study medicine in English in Lithuania?

At LSMU, applications for 2027 to six-year programs such as medicine and dentistry open on 1 November 2026. You need at least B1 English, biology and chemistry at high school, and an entrance exam in biology and chemistry.
