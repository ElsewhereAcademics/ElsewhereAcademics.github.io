Hospitality and Tourism Management

Hospitality and Tourism Management

Hospitality and tourism management studies the structure and operation of the accommodation, food and beverage, travel and events industries. The program combines business knowledge with customer service and teamwork skills.

Business and economics

Example courses

Accommodation and rooms division managementSustainable tourismTrade fair and event managementDigital customer experienceTourism and hospitality policies

What do graduates do?

  • Hotel manager
  • Restaurant manager
  • Event manager
  • Conference center manager
  • Tour manager
  • Travel agency manager
  • Cabin crew member
  • Tourism specialist
IN HIGH SCHOOL

Which subjects matter?

The University of Surrey does not list a compulsory A-level subject for International Hospitality and Tourism Management BSc; it asks for a 4 (C) in GCSE English and mathematics. A-level General Studies and Critical Thinking are not accepted.

Example: University of Surrey, 1st year courses

  1. Services Marketing
  2. Fundamentals of Service Delivery
  3. Tourism Management
  4. Financial Accounting in Service Industries
  5. Rooms Division Management
  6. Responsible and Sustainable Management in the Visitor Economy

Length by country

Netherlands
A bachelor's at a research university (WO) takes 3 years, 180 ECTS. At a university of applied sciences (HBO) it takes 4 years, 240 ECTS.
Germany
At a university, a bachelor's degree takes 3 years, 180 ECTS. At a Fachhochschule it usually takes 3 or 3.5 years, 180 or 210 ECTS.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is there a placement year when studying tourism in the United Kingdom?

Some programs have one. At the University of Surrey, students can choose an optional Professional Training year; in that case the degree becomes 'BSc (Hons) with placement' and takes 4 years.

United Kingdom page Ask your question on WhatsApp
Is a placement part of the training at Swiss hotel schools?

At the two schools we looked at, yes. Swiss Hotel Management School's 3-year Bachelor of Arts program has two paid internships of six months each. Glion's BSc program includes two 24-week internships and a 12-week work placement.
