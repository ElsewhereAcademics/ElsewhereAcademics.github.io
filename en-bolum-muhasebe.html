Accounting

Accounting

Accounting studies the methods used to record, report, audit and interpret the financial information of organizations. Financial reporting, budgeting, taxation and management accounting are core topics of the program.

Example courses

Financial accounting and reportingManagement accountingTaxationCorporate financeBusiness ethics

What do graduates do?

  • Accountant (chartered accountant)
  • Management accountant
  • Independent auditor
  • Forensic accountant
  • Tax adviser
  • Financial analyst
  • Company secretary
  • Management consultant
IN HIGH SCHOOL

Which subjects matter?

Warwick Business School asks for at least an A in A-level maths and a 6 (B) in GCSE English for Accounting and Finance BSc. For applicants with the IB, it expects at least 6 in Higher Level maths.

Example: London School of Economics, first-year courses

  1. AC105 Introduction to Financial Accounting
  2. AC106 Introduction to Management Accounting
  3. FM101 Finance
  4. EC1A3 Microeconomics I
  5. ST107 Quantitative Methods (Statistics)

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does an accounting bachelor's give exemptions from ACCA exams?

ACCA gives exemptions only to programs that it has accredited, and the number of exemptions varies by program. You can check a program's status in ACCA's Exemption calculator tool. Warwick also says that some course choices in Accounting and Finance give exemptions from professional exams.

Can you do a placement year while studying accounting?

In the United Kingdom, there are 4-year options with a placement year. In the University of Liverpool's Accounting and Finance with a Year in Industry, year 3 is one year of work experience in the United Kingdom or abroad. At the University of Reading, the placement year is between year 2 and the final year; 15% of the normal fee is paid for this year.

Does an accounting bachelor's give exemptions in ICAEW's ACA qualification?

In some programs, yes; ICAEW calls this credit for prior learning. The University of Liverpool says its graduates will only complete the remaining modules and the work experience requirement in the ACA. Newman University announced that it received exemption for seven ACA modules for its program. The number of exemptions varies by program.
