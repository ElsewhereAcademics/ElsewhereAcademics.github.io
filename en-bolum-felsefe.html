IN HIGH SCHOOL

Which subjects matter?

For its philosophy bachelor's (BA), UCL asks for AAA at A-level and sets no required subject. It expects at least two of your subjects to be from UCL's list of preferred subjects.

Example: University College London, first-year courses

  1. Ancient Greek Philosophy
  2. Early Modern Philosophy
  3. Knowledge and Reality
  4. Introduction to Logic 1
  5. Introduction to Moral Philosophy
  6. Introduction to Political Philosophy

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is there a philosophy bachelor's in English in Europe?

Yes. Leiden University's Philosophy: Global and Comparative Perspectives bachelor's is in English, is taught in Leiden and is 3 years full-time. The program teaches Western philosophy together with the traditions of thought of India, East Asia, Africa and the Arab world.

Can you study philosophy together with economics?

Yes. LSE's BSc Philosophy and Economics takes 3 years and is taught jointly by two departments. In the final year, public policy is studied from both a philosophy and an economics point of view. Because of the economics courses, mathematics at A-level is required. The standard offer is AAA, with an A in mathematics.

What is PPE at Oxford, and is there an exam when applying?

PPE is a 3-year bachelor's program at Oxford where philosophy, politics and economics are studied together. In the first year you study all three areas, and in later years you can focus on two. All applicants take the three modules of the TARA (Test of Academic Reasoning for Admissions). Mathematics is not required but is recommended.
