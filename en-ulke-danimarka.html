Denmark

In Denmark, bachelor's applications are made through a single system, optagelse.dk. For applicants with a foreign high school diploma, the deadline is 15 March, 12:00. Places are split into two quotas: in quota 1 the high school grade average decides, and in quota 2 the university's own criteria decide.

DENMARK

Studying in Denmark

01

Bachelor's application at one address: optagelse.dk

In Denmark, bachelor's applications are made through optagelse.dk, and the system opens on 1 February. For applicants with a foreign high school diploma, the last day is 15 March, 12:00. Quota 1 looks at the high school grade average and quota 2 looks at the university's own criteria. A student who will receive the diploma in June can add the document by 5 July.

source · source
02

Language requirement: English B and IELTS 6.5

According to Study in Denmark, more than 500 programs are taught in English. At bachelor's and master's level, at least 'English B' level is required, which means IELTS 6.5. Some programs ask for the next level up, 'English A', which equals IELTS 7.0. For Danish-language programs, 'Danish Test 2' or the 'Danish as a Foreign Language' exam is needed.

source · source
03

How many years does it take, and what is the tuition fee?

A bachelor's at a university takes three years and a master's two years. The professional bachelor's given at university colleges takes between three and four and a half years. A student from outside the EU who has no exemption pays a tuition fee. At Aarhus University, the 2026/2027 master's fee is €13,000 or €17,300 a year, depending on the program.

source · source
04

Working while studying

With a student residence permit, you can work at most 90 hours a month during the study period from September to May. In June, July and August, full-time work is allowed. The limit is set monthly, not weekly.

source
05

After graduation: the change of 1 October 2026

For students who apply on or after 1 October 2026, the post-graduation job-seeking permit has been cut from 3 years to 1 year. For those who applied before this date, the period stays at 3 years. According to SIRI, Turkish citizens under the EU-Türkiye Association Agreement may still qualify for the 3-year permit.

source · source

DENMARK

Important for Denmark

01

What is looked at in quota 2 in addition to grades

At Aarhus University, the quota 2 average is calculated only from the grades of subjects related to the program. Activities such as work experience, an internship or a folk high school can raise the evaluation. These activities usually need to last 4 to 12 months and be documented by 15 March. According to Study in Denmark, some institutions also ask for a motivation letter.

02

5 July if the diploma comes in June

The application closes on 15 March, but a student who will receive the high school diploma in June can also apply. The diploma is added to optagelse.dk by 5 July, 12:00.

IN BRIEF

Denmark in brief

Bachelor'sMaster'sGeneral

Last checked: October 2026. Rules and amounts can change. We check the current situation together before you apply.

Bachelor's: length and application route

Bachelor's applications are made through optagelse.dk. For applicants with a foreign high school diploma, the deadline is 15 March, 12:00. Some of the places (quota 1) are given by grade average and some (quota 2) by the criteria the institution publishes.

Master's: length and application route

According to the University of Copenhagen, most master's programs last two years (120 ECTS).

Tuition fees

Students from outside the EU/EEA and Switzerland who are not covered by an exemption pay a tuition fee. For full-program students, the annual fee is about €6,000 to €16,000 (DKK 45,000 to DKK 120,000), depending on the institution.

Student visa

An application for a residence permit is made for the higher education program you were admitted to; the process starts with creating a case order ID and paying the fee. For online applications, biometric data must be given within 14 days at a Danish mission abroad or a SIRI office.

Proof of funds

At the 2026 level, DKK 7,426 a month must be shown, and at most DKK 89,112 for studies longer than a year. If you have paid the first semester's tuition fee, this payment is accepted as proof of sufficient funds.

Working while studying

Students can work up to 90 hours a month during the normal study period from September to May, and full-time in June, July and August.

Work permit after graduation

For applications made on or after 1 October 2026, graduates are generally given a 1-year job-seeking permit; for applications before this date the period was generally 3 years. It is stated that Turkish citizens under the EU-Türkiye Association Agreement may still qualify for the 3-year permit.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

When does the master's application close?

At KU, DTU and Aarhus, the last day for non-EU applicants is 15 January at all three; at KU it is 23:59 (CET). Non-EU applicants who apply to Danish-language programs are also subject to this date.

source · source
How much money do we need to show in the bank?

At the 2026 level, you need to show DKK 7,426 (Danish kroner) a month. For programs longer than a year, the upper limit is DKK 89,112. If you have paid the first semester's tuition fee, SIRI accepts this payment as sufficient proof.

source · source
Can my spouse or family come with me to Denmark?

Family members of students who apply on or after 1 October 2026 can no longer get a residence permit based on the student. SIRI writes that family members of Turkish citizens under the EU-Türkiye Association Agreement may still qualify for a permit. We suggest you ask SIRI about your situation before applying.

source · source
When do we pay the tuition fee?

At Aarhus, payment is made between April and May, and for the first semester by 1 September at the latest. At KU, the fee is set for a 60 ECTS year, and four semesters are paid for a master's.

source · source
Is there a summer school option in Denmark?

Aarhus University's AU Summer University runs in two terms in 2027: 5 to 23 July and 26 July to 13 August 2027. Courses carry ECTS credits and the fee varies by course. AU says some participants need to get a short-stay visa.

source · source
