Law

Law

Legal education studies the rules that govern society and how those rules are interpreted and applied. Core areas such as criminal law, contracts, tort and public law form the base you need to enter the legal professions.

Example courses

Criminal lawContract lawTort lawPublic law and European lawLaw and social justice

What do graduates do?

  • Lawyer (solicitor, barrister)
  • Judge
  • Mediator
  • Arbitrator
  • Trade mark attorney
  • Tax adviser
  • Political risk analyst
  • Human rights lawyer
IN HIGH SCHOOL

Which subjects matter?

UCL does not require any specific A-level subject for the law degree (LLB), but wants at least two subjects to come from UCL's list of preferred subjects. Everyone who applies to UCL for law must take the LNAT.

Example: University College London, first-year courses

  1. Contract
  2. Property I
  3. Public Law
  4. Criminal Law
  5. Laws Connections

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you need to take the YKS to study law abroad?

In most cases, yes. YÖK asks candidates who finished secondary school in Türkiye and will study law abroad to have a rank of at least 100,000 in the EA score type of the YKS (Türkiye's university entrance exam) in the year they start school. The requirement does not apply if the university is in the top 1,000 in at least two of the ARWU, CWTS Leiden, QS and THE rankings in the year of starting. If the requirement is not met, a recognition (denklik) application is not accepted after graduation.

Can someone who studied law abroad become a lawyer in Türkiye?

Yes, but there are a few official steps. First, you get a recognition (denklik) certificate from YÖK. The Attorneyship Law also requires you to pass exams in the courses that are missing compared with law faculties in Türkiye. After these, you need to pass the Law Professions Entrance Exam (HMGS) to start the lawyer's traineeship.

Can there be an extra exam for law recognition (denklik)?

Yes. If YÖK sees gaps in the basic learning outcomes of the program you graduated from, it can ask you to take a level assessment exam. In law, this written exam is held once a year by universities that YÖK designates. You need at least 40 out of 100 to pass.
