Jewellery Design

Jewellery design teaches you to create original jewellery and accessories by combining making skills with design and technical drawing.

Design and architecture

What do graduates do?

  • Jewellery designer
  • Own workshop
  • Freelance work
  • In house designer at manufacturers or retailers
GOOD TO KNOW

Before you apply

A degree is not required, though 3D design, jewellery or silversmithing degrees help; a portfolio is essential.
